Kelly Tisdale: Age, Net Worth, Career, Marriage to Mike Myers, and Life Today

Discover the life, career, and family of scenic artist and entrepreneur Kelly Tisdale, her marriage to comedy icon Mike Myers, children, and net worth.

By Marcus Vance
Culture & Society Features
8 min read October 15, 2026

Kelly Tisdale is an accomplished American scenic artist, entrepreneur, and former restaurateur who is widely recognized as the longtime wife of beloved Canadian-American comedy icon, actor, and filmmaker Mike Myers. While Myers has entertained generations through landmark franchises such as Wayne's World, Austin Powers, and Shrek, Tisdale has carved out her own unique professional journey within the worlds of television production, visual artistry, and progressive Manhattan business ventures.

Unlike many celebrity spouses who embrace the public spotlight, Kelly Tisdale has deliberately maintained a grounded, private lifestyle centered on creative endeavors, family stability, and artistic authenticity. Together with Mike Myers, she has built an enduring marriage spanning more than sixteen years, establishing a peaceful home environment for their three children away from the relentless scrutiny of Hollywood red carpets.

Who Is Kelly Tisdale?

Kelly Tisdale is an American creative professional with a diverse background in scenic design, hospitality management, and independent entrepreneurship. During the early 2000s, she became a prominent fixture in New York City's burgeoning organic food movement as the co-founder and managing partner of TeaNY, a trailblazing vegan café and premium beverage brand launched alongside musician Moby in Manhattan's Lower East Side.

Early Life, Background, and Age

Kelly Tisdale was born on March 27, 1977, in Templeton, Massachusetts. Raised in New England, she developed an early passion for decorative painting, spatial design, and fine arts. Her New England upbringing instilled a deep sense of modesty, self-reliance, and artistic dedication that continued to guide her throughout her adult life and career in New York City.

As of 2026, Kelly Tisdale is 49 years old, celebrating her birthday each year on March 27 under the Aries zodiac sign. Throughout her twenties, Tisdale studied fine arts and specialized production techniques, preparing her for demanding technical roles on major commercial and entertainment sets across the East Coast.

Career as a Scenic Artist and Co-Founder of TeaNY with Moby

Before achieving international recognition as an entrepreneur, Kelly Tisdale established herself as a skilled scenic artist in the television and film industry. In scenic artistry, professionals are tasked with painting backdrops, creating intricate textures, and establishing authentic atmospheric aesthetics for studios and stage productions. Her technical credits include working on television productions such as the late-1990s anthology series Erotic Confessions, where her visual design skills received widespread praise among crew members.

In May 2002, Tisdale took a bold entrepreneurial leap that redefined her public profile. Partnering with longtime friend and electronic music pioneer Richard Melville Hall (better known worldwide as Moby), she co-founded TeaNY, an intimate, stylish tea room and vegan cafe situated at 90 Rivington Street in Manhattan's Lower East Side. At a time when plant-based dining was still relatively niche, TeaNY stood out as an innovative culinary sanctuary, offering over 80 varieties of premium loose-leaf teas, vegan pastries, and cruelty-free sandwiches.

Under Tisdale's hands-on management and artistic vision, TeaNY evolved from a trendy neighborhood hangout into an international beverage distribution brand, selling bottled iced teas and sparkling juices across hundreds of retailers throughout North America and Europe. Tisdale also co-authored Teany Book: Stories, Food, Romance, Cartoons and Hundreds of Teas, a whimsical creative compilation published by Penguin in 2003 that captured the cafe's bohemian spirit. TeaNY operated successfully for over a decade before closing its doors following a fire and shifting urban developments in 2015.

Marriage to Mike Myers: How They Met, Wedding, and Relationship Journey

The romantic story between Kelly Tisdale and Mike Myers began in early 2006. At the time, Myers had recently separated from his first wife, actress and comedy writer Robin Ruzan, after twelve years of marriage. Healing from the personal upheaval of his divorce, Myers found immediate solace and intellectual companionship in Tisdale, whose calm demeanor and creative spirit resonated deeply with him.

The couple dated quietly for roughly four years, purposefully keeping their relationship away from paparazzi and tabloid headlines. Unlike many entertainment romances that unfold through social media and orchestrated press releases, Myers and Tisdale prioritized mutual respect and privacy. Similar to the grounded relationships profiled in our retrospective on celebrity family foundations and creative independent partners, their bond flourished because both partners valued authentic substance over vanity.

In October 2010, Kelly Tisdale and Mike Myers officially married in an intimate, private ceremony held in New York City. The wedding was so discreet that news of their nuptials did not become public until five months later, in March 2011, when Myers' representative officially confirmed the union to media outlets. Friends described the wedding as a heartfelt celebration surrounded only by immediate family and closest confidants.

Children and Family Life: Raising Three Kids Away from the Spotlight

Over the course of their marriage, Kelly Tisdale and Mike Myers have welcomed three children together. Their eldest child, a son named Spike Alan Myers, was born in September 2011. Two years later, in April 2014, the couple welcomed their first daughter, Sunday Molly Myers. Their family was completed in November 2015 with the arrival of their second daughter, Paulina Kathleen Myers.

Mike Myers has frequently spoken about how meeting Kelly and becoming a father completely altered his worldview and career priorities. In numerous candid interviews, the comedy veteran described fatherhood as "the happiest time of my life," explaining that having three young children made him far more selective about accepting demanding film roles that would take him away from home for extended shoots.

Tisdale has played the central role in establishing a stable, grounded household routine for their children. The family splits their time between an elegant apartment in New York City and a peaceful, scenic country retreat in rural Vermont, ensuring their children enjoy an active, nature-oriented childhood rather than an environment dominated by Hollywood expectations.

Kelly Tisdale Net Worth and Financial Standing

As an independent scenic artist, entrepreneur, and author, Kelly Tisdale has built an estimated personal net worth of $1.5 Million to $2 Million. Her wealth was accumulated through her professional work in production design, royalties from the Teany Book, and her equity in the TeaNY brand and beverage distribution network.

When combined with her husband Mike Myers, the household net worth exceeds $200 Million. Over his four-decade career as one of the most bankable comedy creators in cinema history, Myers earned tens of millions from the Austin Powers trilogy, the Shrek animated empire, and lucrative backend producer participations. The couple owns significant high-end real estate assets, including a multi-million-dollar loft in Manhattan's historic SoHo and Tribeca districts, as well as pastoral acreage in Vermont.

Where Is Kelly Tisdale Today?

As of 2026, Kelly Tisdale continues to lead a fulfilling, private life in New York and Vermont alongside Mike Myers and their three children. She remains an active advocate for plant-based nutrition, local community sustainability, and arts education for young children.

While Tisdale occasionally accompanies Myers to major film premieres, charitable galas, and honorary tributes—such as the AFI Life Achievement Awards and film festival screenings—she retains her privacy by abstaining from public social media platforms. Her steady, creative presence continues to serve as the bedrock of Myers' life and family stability.
