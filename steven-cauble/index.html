Who Is Steven Cauble? Inside Lisa Whelchel’s Former Husband’s Ministry and Life Today

A complete biographical overview of Pastor Steven Cauble: his pastoral leadership at The Church on the Way, his 24-year marriage to Facts of Life star Lisa Whelchel, their three children, and life following their 2012 amicable divorce.

By Marcus Vance
Senior Investigative Biographer & Cultural Historian
9 min read November 1, 2026

Steven Cauble is an American Christian pastor, ministry counselor, and author best known publicly as the former husband of acclaimed actress and speaker Lisa Whelchel, who rose to iconic television fame as Blair Warner on NBC's long-running sitcom The Facts of Life. Married for twenty-four years from 1988 to 2012, Cauble served as an associate pastor at The Church on the Way in Van Nuys, California, while co-raising three children.

Unlike entertainment figures who navigate Hollywood celebrity through public red carpets, Cauble dedicated his adult vocation to faith-based community counseling, pastoral education, and family ministry. When he and Whelchel divorced in 2012, both handled the transition with exemplary Christian maturity, remaining close friends and co-parents.

Similar to other steadfast partners who prioritized faith, family privacy, and domestic stability away from entertainment sensationalism—such as Dalia Asafi preserving quiet spiritual convictions alongside a sports legend or Norman Kali choosing family devotion over Hollywood vanity—Steven Cauble has lived a life rooted in substantive faith and personal service.

Pastoral Ministry at The Church on the Way

Steven Cauble entered professional Christian ministry in Southern California during the late 1970s and 1980s. He joined the pastoral team at The Church on the Way, a renowned congregation in Van Nuys founded in 1926 and affiliated with the International Church of the Foursquare Gospel.

Under the leadership of senior pastor Jack Hayford, the church grew into a multi-thousand-member community known for charismatic worship, biblical preaching, and extensive counseling programs. Cauble served as an associate pastor, focusing on young adult discipleship, marriage preparation classes, and community outreach.

Congregants appreciated Cauble’s warm pastoral demeanor, accessible communication style, and biblical integrity. He avoided theatrical pulpit preaching, preferring conversational teaching that addressed the real emotional pressures facing everyday families in greater Los Angeles.

Meeting Lisa Whelchel and Their 1988 Marriage

In the mid-1980s, Lisa Whelchel was one of the most recognizable young stars on American television. Having portrayed preppy student Blair Warner on The Facts of Life since 1979, she sought genuine spiritual fellowship away from studio lots and began attending The Church on the Way.

Lisa met Pastor Steven Cauble through the church's young adult prayer meetings. Despite an age difference of roughly twelve years, the two connected deeply through shared Christian faith, mutual respect for scripture, and a desire for a peaceful family life.

On July 9, 1988—shortly after The Facts of Life concluded its historic nine-season television run—Steven and Lisa married at The Church on the Way in front of family, fellow congregants, and close cast members. Following their wedding, Lisa made the conscious decision to step away from full-time Hollywood acting to focus on marriage and motherhood.

Family Life, Homeschooling, and Raising Three Children

Steven and Lisa welcomed three children in rapid succession: son Tucker in 1990, daughter Haven in 1991, and daughter Clancy in 1992. The Cauble household was organized around classical homeschooling and Christian fellowship.

Lisa became a bestselling Christian author and keynote speaker on parenting, publishing popular books like Creative Correction and The Facts of Life (and Other Lessons My Father Taught Me). Throughout this period, Steven provided vital pastoral guidance and structural support, ensuring their home remained a calm sanctuary for their young children.

All three children flourished academically and creatively. Son Tucker grew into music production, daughter Haven pursued counseling and ministry, and youngest daughter Clancy followed creative performing arts while maintaining strong faith foundations.

Amicable 2012 Divorce and Continued Family Friendship

In March 2012, after nearly twenty-four years of marriage, Steven Cauble and Lisa Whelchel finalized their divorce. The announcement came as a surprise to many in the Christian community, as the couple had long been viewed as a pillar of stability.

However, unlike typical celebrity breakups marred by tabloid accusations or financial disputes, Steven and Lisa modeled extraordinary grace. In public statements following her appearance on CBS's Survivor: Philippines in late 2012, Lisa explained that while their romantic partnership had concluded, their commitment to one another as family remained absolute.

The former couple continued to spend major holidays together, celebrate their children's weddings side by side, and communicate with warmth. Steven has consistently declined commercial media interviews, choosing to maintain total privacy and respect for his family.

Pastoral Counseling Principles and Conflict Resolution Frameworks

Throughout his ministry career in Southern California, Steven Cauble earned the trust of countless families through his grounded approach to interpersonal conflict. He avoided rigid dogmatic scolding, choosing instead to employ active empathetic listening, emotional de-escalation, and structured biblical counseling.

He regularly facilitated premarital workshops designed to help young couples navigate financial planning, career ambitions, and spiritual differences before exchanging wedding vows. Many of the couples who sat in his office described his counseling sessions as compassionate, fair-minded, and intensely focused on building lasting mutual respect.

These same personal principles guided his own conduct when his twenty-four-year marriage to Lisa Whelchel faced private difficulties. By refusing to let emotional disagreement turn into public acrimony, Cauble demonstrated that Christian grace applies just as powerfully during times of personal transition as it does during times of joy.

Mentorship of Younger Ministry Leaders and Life in California Today

In his later years, Pastor Cauble transitioned from frontline pulpit duties to one-on-one leadership mentorship. He advises emerging Christian ministers, offering practical wisdom on managing pastoral burnout, maintaining healthy work-life boundaries, and serving local communities with humility.

He continues to reside in Southern California, enjoying regular visits with his three adult children and grandchildren. Friends describe him as a peaceful, reflective scholar who spends his mornings in personal study and prayer, fully content with a quiet, service-driven life away from cameras.

Where Steven Cauble Is Today and Net Worth

Today, Steven Cauble is approximately 75 years old and continues to reside in California. He remains active in pastoral mentoring, offering counsel to younger ministers and consulting with independent Christian organizations.

His individual net worth is estimated to be between $500,000 and $1 million, earned from decades of pastoral service, ministerial counseling, and faith-based writing. Former wife Lisa Whelchel remarried psychologist Pete Harris in 2019, with Steven offering his blessings.

Much like other inspirational Christian authors and educators featured in our biography library—such as Katherine Norland sharing biblical encouragement—Steven Cauble’s legacy is defined by lifelong spiritual consistency and peaceful family leadership.
