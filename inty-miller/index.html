Inty Miller: How Master P’s Daughter Built Her Own Path in Music, Her Age, Career, and Net Worth

An in-depth look at Inty Miller, also known as Princess Inty—her music releases, creative writing, age, family legacy within the No Limit dynasty, and net worth.

By Sarah Jenkins
Senior Biographical Correspondent
9 min read October 18, 2026

In the history of independent American hip-hop, few movements match the monumental triumph of No Limit Records. Founded in New Orleans by Percy "Master P" Miller, the label shattered music industry conventions, sold tens of millions of records independently, and birthed a multi-generational entertainment dynasty. Growing up at the very center of that cultural powerhouse was Inty Miller—an artist, writer, and entrepreneur who has steadily shaped her own distinctive creative path.

Known in the music world as "Princess Inty," she absorbed the relentless work ethic of her father and siblings while refusing to rely merely on inherited celebrity. Balancing melodic hip-hop releases with published literary projects and wellness advocacy, Inty represents the modern evolution of the Miller family legacy—creative, self-directed, and fiercely resilient.

Inty Miller Age, Birthplace, and Growing Up in New Orleans

Intylyana Miller was born on February 27, 1993, in New Orleans, Louisiana. She arrived just as her father was building No Limit Records from a neighborhood brick-and-mortar record store into an unstoppable national music juggernaut. As of 2026, Inty Miller is 33 years old.

Growing up alongside her brothers and sisters—including Romeo, Hercy, Mercy, Italy, Veno, and Cymphonique—Inty experienced a unique childhood steeped in creative hustle. While her family relocated between Southern California, Houston, and New Orleans as business interests expanded, her parents placed a strict emphasis on formal education, religious faith, and self-discipline. From elementary school through high school, Inty was encouraged to develop her own passions beyond the recording booth.

The Miller home was frequently filled with master audio tapes, video storyboards, and marketing meetings. Master P's historic 85-15 distribution agreement with Priority Records in the mid-1990s—which allowed No Limit to retain 100% of its masters while receiving major distribution—became legendary in business schools. Observing these high-level negotiations gave Inty an intuitive grasp of copyright ownership and intellectual property that few young artists possess.

The No Limit Legacy and Musical Evolution as Princess Inty

Spending her youth in high-end recording studios naturally honed Inty's ear for rhythm, cadence, and songwriting. Rather than rushing into the industry at a young age like her brother Romeo, Inty took time to cultivate her distinct sound. Adopting the stage moniker Princess Inty, she emerged with a style blending Southern hip-hop bounce with introspective contemporary R&B melodies.

Her standout tracks, including "That Real Love" and "For Better or Worse," resonated with listeners for their emotional authenticity, relatable relationship narratives, and crisp production. Music critics noted that her vocal delivery carried a natural warmth and confidence, free from the repetitive formulas of mainstream radio rap. Much like the independent artistic spirits chronicled in our analyses of Samantha Marie Olit and Marisela Vallejos Felix, Inty's art is deeply rooted in personal integrity and creative truth.

In the studio, Inty often works with close producers who understand her preference for live instrumentation, acoustic guitars, and rhythmic 808 percussion. Her lyricism often touches on self-worth, emotional maturity, and the journey of navigating young adulthood under a celebrated family banner.

Creative Writing, Authorship, and Independent Business Ventures

Beyond the recording studio, Inty has devoted significant energy to creative writing and literary expression. Blessed with a reflective mind, she has authored poetry, reflective essays, and motivational journals aimed at young women navigating the pressures of family expectations, social media culture, and personal self-esteem.

In addition to her writing, Inty has explored digital retail and lifestyle branding. Drawing inspiration from her father's legendary entrepreneurial mindset—which expanded No Limit from music into film, athletics, food products, and real estate—Inty has launched boutique e-commerce ventures and lifestyle merchandising lines that promote self-love, mental wellness, and artistic expression.

Her literary chapbooks emphasize that genuine healing begins when individuals confront vulnerability rather than masking emotions behind outward material success. This honest message has garnered her a dedicated following of young readers seeking practical, empathetic guidance.

Beyond publishing, Inty has actively collaborated with local nonprofit community centers in New Orleans and Los Angeles, hosting creative writing and music composition clinics for teenage girls. Through these interactive sessions, she demystifies the mechanics of intellectual property, publishing splits, and self-expression, empowering a new generation to see themselves as legitimate creative owners rather than mere performers.

Family Dynamics, Sibling Bonds, and Navigating Shared Loss

The Miller family has long been celebrated for their tight-knit solidarity. Through triumphs and public challenges, the siblings have supported one another across collegiate athletics, television productions, and music tours. However, in May 2022, the family was devastated by the heartbreaking passing of Inty's younger sister, Tytyana Miller, at the age of 29.

The profound grief deeply impacted Inty, who was exceptionally close to Tytyana. In the wake of the tragedy, Inty and her family transformed their pain into purposeful action, speaking openly about mental health struggles, substance abuse awareness, and the vital necessity of compassionate family support systems. Her courageous transparency has offered hope and solace to thousands of families experiencing similar trials across the nation.

Through community events and memorial initiatives, Inty has consistently honored Tytyana's spirit, emphasizing that family unity and emotional openness are far more valuable than commercial achievements or public accolades.

Inty Miller Net Worth and Financial Autonomy

As of 2026, Inty Miller has an estimated personal net worth of $500,000 to $1 Million. While her father Master P possesses a multi-million-dollar empire estimated at over $200 million, Inty has consistently prioritized earning her own income through streaming music royalties, literary publishing, digital partnerships, and commercial design ventures.

By establishing her own business LLCs and maintaining ownership of her master recordings and publishing copyrights, Inty adheres closely to the foundational No Limit business philosophy: ownership is the true key to generational wealth and creative freedom.

Advocacy, Wellness, and Future Horizons in 2026

Today, Inty Miller continues to create and inspire from her base in California. With upcoming musical singles in development, plans for an expanded memoir-style book, and ongoing community workshops centered on youth mental health and wellness, she has firmly established herself as a multi-dimensional voice for her generation.

Inty proves that carrying a legendary last name does not require living in its shadow. With talent, perseverance, and genuine compassion, she is honoring her roots while writing her own inspiring chapter in the American creative story.
