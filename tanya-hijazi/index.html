Tanya Hijazi: What to Know About Rick James’ Former Wife, Her Age, Marriage, Son Tazman, and Life Today

In the colorful, turbulent annals of American funk and soul music, few figures cast as flamboyant a shadow as the legendary "King of Punk Funk," Rick James. The mastermind behind groundbreaking hits like "Super Freak," "Give It to Me Baby," and "Mary Jane," James lived a life marked by staggering artistic highs and harrowing personal trials. Yet through the most transformative chapter of his later years—including his sobriety journey, incarceration, and eventual artistic resurgence—one woman stood firmly beside him: his longtime partner and former wife, Tanya Anne Hijazi.

Decades after their 2002 divorce and James’s passing in 2004, public fascination with Tanya Hijazi remains significant. Who Is Tanya Hijazi?

Tanya Hijazi is an American media personality, former performer, and businesswoman who gained international prominence during the 1990s as the partner and subsequent wife of Grammy Award-winning funk pioneer Rick James. Possessing striking features, charisma, and musical passion, Tanya shared a deep emotional bond with James that spanned more than a decade.

Much like other figures in musical history whose lives intersected with iconic creators during difficult periods—such as Laurie Holmond with Snoop Dogg or Jacqueline Ray with Tom Selleck—Tanya’s life is defined by resilience, survival, and a determination to build an honorable, private existence away from the intense media storms of her youth.

Early Background, Upbringing, and Approximate Age

Tanya Anne Hijazi was born in 1972 in the United States. As of 2026, Tanya is 54 years old. Raised during the 1970s and 1980s, she demonstrated an early affinity for performing arts, fashion, and urban music culture.

By her late teens, Tanya moved to California, where she became active in the vibrant Los Angeles entertainment and music scene. Her vibrant energy and striking appearance brought her into social circles populated by prominent recording artists, producers, and nightlife luminaries during the golden era of West Coast urban music.

Meeting Rick James and Their Tumultuous Early Years

In 1989, at seventeen years old, Tanya met Rick James at a party in California. At the time, James was already a legendary, multi-platinum star, though he was privately battling severe substance abuse issues and the psychological burdens of sustained celebrity. Despite their significant age difference, the two formed an immediate, intense connection that quickly developed into a serious romantic relationship.

The early 1990s brought profound personal challenges for both Tanya and Rick. Entangled in the excesses of the Hollywood party circuit, the couple became embroiled in highly publicized legal troubles in 1991. The experience served as a sobering wake-up call. Both underwent mandatory legal consequences and rehabilitative treatment, using the experience to sever ties with toxic lifestyle habits and refocus on personal accountability and health.

Marriage in 1996 and Family Life With Son Tazman

In 1993, amidst their personal recovery journey, Tanya gave birth to their son, Tazman James. The arrival of Tazman provided both Tanya and Rick with profound renewed purpose. Determined to solidify their family unit, Rick James proposed, and the couple married on Christmas Eve, December 24, 1996, at an intimate ceremony in Los Angeles.

Following their wedding, Tanya and Rick worked diligently to establish a stable domestic routine. They collaborated on music management, supported each other through sobriety milestones, and celebrated Rick’s gradual return to the recording studio and live concert stages. Friends from that era remember Tanya as a fiercely loyal partner who fought hard to keep Rick healthy, grounded, and focused on his musical gifts.

The 'Urban Rapsody' Era and Resurgence in the Late 1990s

During the late 1990s, Tanya played a crucial behind-the-scenes role during the creation and promotional tour of Rick James’s comeback album, Urban Rapsody (1997). Released on Private I Records with production assistance from veteran arranger Narada Michael Walden, the record showcased a more mature, contemplative vocal delivery from James. Tanya accompanied Rick to recording sessions at major Los Angeles and New York studios, serving as a trusted advisor on wardrobe, scheduling, and personal logistics.

Their joint appearances on national television broadcasts and industry trade gatherings demonstrated that their commitment had withstood the severe turbulence of earlier years. Music critics at the time praised the newfound stability in James’s creative output, frequently acknowledging Tanya’s steadfast loyalty as a vital stabilizing influence during his mid-career creative revival.

The 2002 Divorce and Moving Past the Spotlight

Despite their enduring love, the long-term emotional scars from their early trials and the physical health challenges confronting Rick proved too difficult to sustain their romantic union. In 2002, after six years of marriage and thirteen years together, Tanya and Rick mutually filed for divorce, which was finalized in December 2002.

Crucially, their separation remained completely amicable. Unlike bitter celebrity divorces, Tanya and Rick remained close friends and co-parents to Tazman. When Rick James tragically passed away on August 6, 2004, at the age of 56 from heart failure, Tanya was deeply grieved. She attended his memorial services alongside his other children and fellow musicians, honoring the man she had loved through both triumph and adversity.

Son Tazman James and Honoring the Musical Legacy

Following James's passing, Tanya’s highest priority remained the protection and education of their son, Tazman James. Now an adult in his thirties, Tazman has pursued creative audio production and visual arts, honoring his father’s extraordinary musical heritage while maintaining a grounded, respectful lifestyle.

Tanya has occasionally participated in reputable retrospective projects—including acclaimed music documentaries exploring Rick James’s artistic genius—offering balanced, empathetic insights into the complex human being behind the "Super Freak" persona. Her testimony has helped provide historical depth, ensuring that audiences appreciate James’s songwriting brilliance and humanity rather than merely sensationalist headlines.
