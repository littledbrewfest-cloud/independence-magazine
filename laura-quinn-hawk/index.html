Who Is Laura Quinn Hawk? Interior Designer, Marriage to A.J. Hawk, and Family Life

A comprehensive biography of Laura Quinn Hawk: her prominent football lineage, viral split-jersey moment, flourishing interior design business, and two-decade marriage to A.J. Hawk.

By Sarah Jenkins
Senior Lifestyle & Culture Writer
9 min read October 24, 2026

In the annals of American college football lore, few casual spectators have achieved spontaneous, affectionate national recognition quite like Laura Quinn Hawk did on January 2, 2006. Sitting in the sun-drenched stands of Sun Devil Stadium during the Fiesta Bowl, she captured the television cameras wearing a bespoke, custom-sewn jersey split directly down the middle—half scarlet and gray for her then-boyfriend, Ohio State All-American linebacker A.J. Hawk, and half blue and gold for her brother, Notre Dame starting quarterback Brady Quinn. Yet while that memorable broadcast image cemented her place in pop-culture sports history, Laura Quinn Hawk's enduring narrative is one of entrepreneurial talent, architectural taste, and an exemplary twenty-year marriage anchored in family life in Ohio.

Similar to dedicated partners who cultivate flourishing creative enterprises alongside high-profile athletic careers, such as Mirka Federer shaping family ventures with Roger Federer or Cathleen Oveson maintaining domestic warmth alongside Kenny Johnson, Laura Quinn Hawk has established an authentic, independent professional identity.

Early Life, Dublin Roots, and a Prominent Football Family

Laura Quinn was born in 1983 in Dublin, Ohio, an affluent suburb northwest of Columbus known for its strong community traditions and deep Midwestern athletic culture. She was born to Tyrone Quinn, a former Vietnam War veteran, and Robin Quinn. Growing up in Dublin alongside her brother Brady Quinn and sister Kelly Quinn, sports and competitive discipline were woven into everyday family life.

While her younger brother Brady emerged as a football sensation at Dublin Coffman High School before becoming an iconic quarterback at the University of Notre Dame, Laura pursued artistic and creative interests. Possessing an instinctive visual eye for spatial harmony, color coordination, and structural balance, she developed an early ambition to transform living spaces through purposeful interior design.

Education at Cal State Northridge and Transition to Interior Architecture

Seeking to develop formal technical skills away from the Midwestern football fishbowl, Quinn relocated to Southern California, enrolling at California State University, Northridge (CSUN). In Los Angeles, she studied interior design, architectural drafting, textile science, and spatial composition.

Her time on the West Coast broadened her stylistic sensibilities, exposing her to modern coastal aesthetics, California minimalist concepts, and mid-century modern architectural frameworks. After completing her studies, Quinn returned to the Midwest equipped with both formal credentials and a sophisticated design philosophy that would eventually blossom into her private design firm, LCH Interiors.

The Famous Split-Jersey Moment and Meeting A.J. Hawk

The courtship between Laura Quinn and A.J. Hawk began during their college years through mutual friends in the Columbus area. Hawk was dominating college football as a consensus All-American linebacker for the Ohio State Buckeyes under Jim Tressel, while Brady Quinn was steering Notre Dame's offense under Charlie Weis.

When the two storied programs met in the prestigious 2006 Fiesta Bowl in Tempe, Arizona, Laura faced a classic dilemma of divided loyalties: support her brother, the Fighting Irish quarterback, or her boyfriend, the Buckeyes defensive captain. Rather than choosing, she commissioned a custom seamstress to stitch together a one-of-a-kind jersey: the left half bearing Quinn's blue-and-gold #10 and the right half displaying Hawk's scarlet-and-gray #47. The clever fashion statement was repeatedly highlighted on ESPN's broadcast, earning nationwide applause for wit, loyalty, and sportsmanship. (Ohio State won the matchup 34-20, though both men went on to become first-round NFL draft selections that spring).

Marriage in Columbus, Family Life, and Raising Four Children

Shortly before A.J. Hawk was drafted fifth overall by the Green Bay Packers in the 2006 NFL Draft, the couple solidified their commitment with a quiet civil ceremony in March 2006. A year later, on March 17, 2007 (St. Patrick's Day), they held a full traditional religious wedding ceremony and lavish reception at the historic St. Paul the Apostle Catholic Church and the Renaissance Columbus Downtown Hotel, surrounded by hundreds of family members, collegiate teammates, and NFL colleagues.

Throughout Hawk's nine-season tenure in Green Bay—which included winning Super Bowl XLV in 2011—Laura established a peaceful home base. Together, the couple has raised four children: daughters Lennon Noel and Dietrich, and sons Hendrix and their youngest boy. The family prioritizes a grounded Midwestern upbringing, emphasizing humility, academic curiosity, and private community engagement over celebrity excess.

Building LCH Interiors: Custom Residential Design in Ohio

Rather than simply resting on her husband's substantial NFL contracts, Laura Hawk channeled her creative passion into founding LCH Interiors. Operating out of Central Ohio, her firm specializes in bespoke luxury residential remodeling, spatial restructuring, custom cabinetry planning, and organic interior styling.

Clients praise her ability to combine high-end elegance with practical, family-friendly durability. Her design philosophy embraces warm natural lighting, earthy textural materials, and balanced neutrals that create calm, welcoming living environments. Today, LCH Interiors continues to take on selective residential projects throughout the Midwest, earning respected architectural credibility across Ohio's premier communities.

Community Philanthropy and Hawk's Locks for Kids

Throughout their time in Green Bay and Ohio, Laura and A.J. Hawk placed significant emphasis on civic contribution. Among their most visible charitable endeavors was Hawk's Locks for Kids, a philanthropic foundation established to support children experiencing hair loss due to chemotherapy and other medical conditions. Laura helped coordinate fundraising galas, donor logistics, and community hair donation events, rallying support from both Packers fans and Ohio State alumni.

The Hawk family has also been long-time supporters of the Columbus-based Buckeye Cruise for Cancer, helping generate millions of dollars for the Urban and Shelley Meyer Fund for Cancer Research. Friends and neighbors in Dublin frequently note that despite their public visibility and athletic acclaim, Laura and A.J. maintain an exceptionally approachable, humble posture within the local school district and civic organizations.

Architectural Style and the Design Vision of LCH Interiors

At LCH Interiors, Laura Quinn Hawk's design methodology focuses on timeless durability rather than fleeting digital trends. Recognizing the functional demands of active families with young children and pets, her residential projects emphasize stain-resistant natural textiles, engineered European oak flooring, custom storage millwork, and balanced architectural lighting. Her work proves that a luxury home can simultaneously look magazine-worthy and comfortably withstand the energetic daily realities of family life.

Laura Quinn Hawk Age, Net Worth Overview, and Public Profile

As of 2026, Laura Quinn Hawk is approximately 43 years old. Hawk's eleven-year NFL playing career and his prominent media role on The Pat McAfee Show—comfortably surpasses $20 million.
