Inside the Story of Laurie Holmond: Snoop Dogg’s High School Partner, Her Age, Son Julian, and Quiet Life

From Doggystyle to Olympic broadcast hosting, his brand has achieved rare global ubiquity. Yet connected to his early life in Southern California is Laurie Holmond—his high school sweetheart, longtime confidante, and the devoted mother of his son, Julian Corrie Broadus.

Who Is Laurie Holmond?

Laurie Holmond is an American medical administration specialist best known to the public as the former partner of hip-hop pioneer Snoop Dogg and the mother of his son, Julian Corrie Broadus. Born and raised in Long Beach, California, Holmond knew Snoop Dogg before his meteoric rise to global music stardom.

While the circumstances of their relationship drew occasional tabloid interest during the late 1990s and early 2000s, Holmond earned widespread admiration for handling family matters with maturity, discretion, and quiet strength. She chose not to pursue celebrity culture, focusing instead on her professional work in healthcare clinics and raising her son with strong personal values.

Early Years in Long Beach and Her Exact Age

Laurie Holmond was born in 1973 in Long Beach, California. Growing up in the vibrant, culturally rich coastal city of Los Angeles County, she attended Long Beach Polytechnic High School—the historic institution that also educated future hip-hop stars Calvin Broadus Jr., Warren G, and Cameron Diaz. As of 2026, Laurie Holmond is 53 years old.

During her adolescence, Holmond was known among classmates for her warm personality, practical mindset, and level-headed temperament. Long Beach in the late 1980s was a vibrant hub of athletic and creative youth culture, yet Holmond remained focused on practical life goals. She maintained close ties with childhood friends and pursued post-secondary vocational training in medical administration and healthcare customer services.

Her family background emphasized educational achievement, personal accountability, and community solidarity. These early life principles became the bedrock of her character, enabling her to stay remarkably grounded even as peers and acquaintances ascended to global musical stardom.

Her Relationship With Snoop Dogg (Calvin Broadus Jr.)

Laurie Holmond and Calvin Broadus Jr. first connected during their high school years in Long Beach in the late 1980s. Their connection predated the multi-platinum albums, Death Row Records signings, and worldwide fame that would soon redefine Broadus’s life. Holmond knew Calvin as an aspiring young lyricist and charismatic local athlete before he adopted the moniker Snoop Doggy Dogg.

Although Broadus married his high school sweetheart Shante Taylor in 1997, his deep emotional history with Holmond led to a reunion during a turbulent period in 1997, resulting in the birth of their son Julian in 1998. When news of Julian's parentage became public several years later, Holmond handled legal establishment of paternity through formal family court proceedings with quiet professionalism, ensuring that legal child support and parental responsibilities were formalized without unnecessary public hostility.

Welcoming Son Julian Corrie Broadus and Co-Parenting Journey

In 1998, Holmond gave birth to Julian Corrie Broadus. From his earliest years, Holmond dedicated herself to giving Julian a grounded, secure upbringing. While Broadus was frequently away touring the world and headlining stadium concerts, Holmond provided a structured daily routine, prioritizing Julian's schooling, athletic activities, and emotional security.

As Julian grew into his teenage years, Snoop Dogg stepped forward to build an active, loving father-son relationship with him, welcoming Julian into the broader Broadus family circle. In interviews, Julian has repeatedly praised his mother for being his ultimate foundation, teaching him discipline, humility, and the importance of forging an independent identity rather than coasting on his father's legendary reputation.

Julian Broadus Today: Music, Modeling, and Real Estate

Today, Julian Corrie Broadus is an accomplished adult who has explored multiple creative and professional avenues. He studied at Bowling Green State University, where he demonstrated talent on the basketball court, before pursuing his interests in contemporary R&B music, fashion modeling, and high-end residential real estate.

Julian has released original music singles, worked with prominent lifestyle brands, and served as a luxury real estate agent with prestigious Southern California brokerages. Career as a Medical Receptionist and Private Lifestyle

Throughout her adult life, Laurie Holmond has worked steadily in the healthcare and clinical administrative sector. As a medical receptionist and patient coordinator in California clinics, she manages patient appointments, insurance verifications, and clinical records with care and confidentiality.

In community healthcare clinics, administrative coordinators serve as the crucial frontline bridge between medical providers, diagnostic laboratories, and patient families. Holmond's meticulous attention to patient privacy protocols, scheduling workflows, and empathetic patient intake has made her a highly valued team member across medical practices.

Her colleagues have long commended her dependable work ethic and genuine compassion for patients. Despite the public curiosity that occasionally surfaces regarding her connection to hip-hop royalty, Holmond has never used her personal history for commercial gain, finding satisfaction in honest professional labor and quiet domestic peace.

Laurie Holmond Net Worth and Living Away From the Cameras

As of 2026, Laurie Holmond possesses an estimated personal net worth of $300,000 to $500,000. This wealth has been built through decades of steady employment in the medical administration sector, sensible savings, and child support agreements established during Julian's youth.

Holmond continues to live quietly in Southern California, staying completely away from reality television and tabloid interviews. When not working, she enjoys tending her garden, attending local church services, cooking family dinners, and supporting local youth sports programs in Los Angeles County. Her life is filled with the joys of family, close friends, and watching her son achieve his own professional goals. Her enduring story is a reminder of the quiet courage of everyday mothers who put their children's well-being first.
