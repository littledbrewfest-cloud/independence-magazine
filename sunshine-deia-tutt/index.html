Sunshine Deia Tutt: What to Know About Chris Kattan’s Former Wife, Her Age, Career, and Life Today

A comprehensive profile of model, actress, and interior designer Sunshine Deia Tutt: her relationship and marriage with SNL veteran Chris Kattan, creative career, and current pursuits.

By Sarah Jenkins
Senior Lifestyle & Culture Writer
9 min read October 20, 2026

When comedian and Saturday Night Live legend Chris Kattan announced his engagement in late 2006, entertainment media outlets turned their spotlight toward his fiancée, Sunshine Deia Tutt. A striking Texas native who had worked as a print model, commercial actress, and interior design consultant, Tutt possessed an effortless grace that contrasted pleasantly with Kattan’s energetic comedic persona. Though their 2008 marriage proved brief, lasting less than two months before an amicable separation, public interest in Tutt's life, career transition, and exact biographical background has persisted for nearly two decades.

Rather than capitalizing on her high-profile Hollywood connection to chase reality television fame, Sunshine Deia Tutt made the conscious choice to step away from entertainment red carpets. Instead, she channeled her visual creativity and spatial acuity into high-end residential interior design and boutique architecture, establishing an independent professional reputation defined by quality and discretion.

Who Is Sunshine Deia Tutt?

Sunshine Deia Tutt is an American interior designer, former commercial model, and screen actress who became a national media figure in the late 2000s during her high-profile courtship and subsequent marriage to comedic actor Chris Kattan. Known for her natural photogenic presence and southern warmth, Tutt made notable screen appearances before intentionally refocusing her career on bespoke architectural aesthetics and private client design services.

Much like other creative partners of entertainment industry figures—such as Kelly Tisdale (spouse of Mike Myers) and Rishia Haas—Sunshine demonstrated that public exposure does not have to dictate a professional lifetime, pivoting smoothly into specialized entrepreneurship.

Early Life, Texas Upbringing, and Exact Age

Sunshine Deia Tutt was born on September 23, 1976, in Brownsville, Texas. As of 2026, Sunshine is 50 years old. Raised in the warm coastal climate of South Texas, she grew up in an encouraging environment where she demonstrated an early talent for drawing, spatial planning, and home decoration.

After completing high school in Texas, Tutt enrolled at the University of Texas at Austin, where she studied design, visual communications, and architectural history. Her refined aesthetic sensibility, paired with her tall, striking appearance, soon drew inquiries from regional and national modeling scouts, leading her to move to Los Angeles to explore opportunities in commercial advertising and film production.

Career Transition: From Modeling and Acting to High-End Interior Design

In Los Angeles during the late 1990s and early 2000s, Sunshine Deia Tutt worked steadily as a commercial model for apparel brands, lifestyle publications, and national advertising campaigns. Her screen work included small film appearances, most notably playing the role of "Theatre Holding Actress" in the 2001 dark fantasy comedy Monkeybone, directed by Henry Selick and starring Brendan Fraser, Bridget Fonda, and Whoopi Goldberg.

While she enjoyed the creative collaboration on film sets, Tutt realized her true passion lay in tangible physical spaces rather than the transient nature of auditions. She increasingly focused her energies on residential design, working with boutique architectural firms in Los Angeles and Malibu to curate bespoke furnishings, custom lighting, and modern sustainable living spaces for private homeowners.

Relationship and Marriage with Comedian Chris Kattan

In early 2005, Sunshine Deia Tutt was introduced to Chris Kattan, one of the most recognizable stars from NBC’s legendary late-night sketch show Saturday Night Live and co-creator of the iconic Doug and Steve Butabi characters from A Night at the Roxbury. The pair connected over a shared sense of humor, mutual friends, and a love for California’s outdoor lifestyle, beginning a serious relationship that lasted several years.

On Christmas Eve in 2006, Kattan proposed to Tutt at her grandparents' home in Gainesville, Texas, presenting her with a custom antique-style diamond engagement ring. The couple spent over a year planning their nuptials, opting for an elegant ceremony in northern California. On June 28, 2008, Sunshine and Chris were married in Oakhurst, California, near Yosemite National Park, in front of approximately 150 family members and friends, including numerous comedy industry colleagues.

The Separation, Divorce Agreement, and Moving Forward

Despite their strong mutual affection and a romantic courtship, the day-to-day reality of married life quickly exposed fundamental differences in lifestyle expectations and personal paths. Just eight weeks after their Yosemite wedding, on August 10, 2008, the couple made the difficult decision to separate.

The subsequent divorce proceedings remained remarkably respectful and civilized. Avoidant of tabloid headlines or courtroom warfare, Kattan and Tutt worked with their attorneys to reach an amicable settlement. Kattan agreed to provide a structured financial equalization settlement, and their divorce was finalized in February 2009. Both parties spoke graciously about each other in the aftermath, wishing one another genuine happiness and creative success.

Sunshine Deia Tutt’s Net Worth and Professional Success

As of 2026, Sunshine Deia Tutt’s estimated net worth is between $500,000 and $800,000. Unlike many former celebrity spouses who rely on permanent spousal support, Tutt channeled her financial settlement directly into her independent design business, establishing a sustainable consultancy that generates continuous revenue through bespoke residential renovation projects.

Her design firm caters to discerning private homeowners throughout California, focusing on organic materials, mid-century architectural restoration, and elegant neutral palettes. Her reputation in the interior design sector is built on rigorous execution, word-of-mouth client referrals, and quiet professional dependability.

Life Beyond Hollywood: Where Is Sunshine Deia Tutt Today?

Today, at 50 years of age, Sunshine Deia Tutt lives a private, fulfilling life centered on design innovation, wellness, and artistic exploration. She maintains minimal public social media visibility, opting to conduct her business through private client portals and personal referrals.

Free from the pressures of celebrity commentary, Tutt has crafted a life of independence and purpose. Her trajectory proves that life after a brief, intense encounter with Hollywood celebrity can be grounded, prosperous, and defined strictly by one’s own talent and choices.
