Inside the Life of Diane Lou Oswald: Woody Harrelson’s Mother, Her Age, Career, and Inspiring Story

An in-depth look at Diane Lou Oswald: her resilient journey raising three boys as a single mother, career as a legal secretary, and bond with actor Woody Harrelson.

By Marcus Vance
Culture & Society Features
8 min read October 17, 2026

Behind the extraordinary, multifaceted career of three-time Academy Award nominee and Emmy-winning actor Woody Harrelson lies the profound influence of a woman whose courage and moral clarity defined his existence: his mother, Diane Lou Oswald. Long before Woody captured the hearts of global audiences in Cheers, White Men Can't Jump, True Detective, and Three Billboards Outside Ebbing, Missouri, Diane was single-handedly steering her three young boys through profound adversity in the American Midwest.

Faced with the sudden abandonment and subsequent criminal trials of her ex-husband, Diane Lou Oswald refused to let hardship dictate her family's destiny. Working long, punishing hours as a dedicated legal secretary in southwestern Ohio, she provided unconditional love, intellectual curiosity, and deep spiritual ethics that anchored her sons throughout their lives.

The Woman Behind Woody Harrelson's Success

Diane Lou Oswald represents a generation of resilient American working mothers who sacrificed personal ambitions to guarantee a brighter future for their children. While celebrity profiles often focus exclusively on famous actors, Woody Harrelson has never hesitated to credit his mother as the primary architect of his worldview, personal empathy, and lifelong commitment to peace and environmental stewardship.

Early Life in Ohio, Roots, and Age in 2026

Diane Lou Oswald was born in March 1937 in the historic city of Lebanon, located in Warren County, Ohio. She was raised in a principled Midwestern household where community service, modest living, and Presbyterian church attendance were cornerstones of daily life. As of 2026, Diane Lou Oswald is 89 years old.

Growing up in Lebanon, Ohio, Diane was immersed in a tight-knit Midwestern community where neighbors supported one another through economic shifts. Her father, an industrious local craftsman, and her mother instilled in her a profound work ethic that prioritized honesty above material wealth. When Diane returned to Lebanon with her young sons after leaving Texas, the community provided a safe haven where Woody, Brett, and Jordan could attend local schools without the intrusive stigma of their father's notoriety.

During Woody's years at Lebanon High School, Diane worked tirelessly to balance strict maternal discipline with creative freedom. Recognizing Woody's natural gifts for comedy, athletic agility in football and track, and theatrical expression, she actively encouraged his participation in school plays and speech competitions. When Woody was accepted into Hanover College in Indiana to study theatre and English, Diane managed her modest legal secretarial salary to help cover essential textbooks and tuition fees, ensuring her son had every opportunity she herself had sacrificed.

During her youth, Diane distinguished herself through exceptional organizational skills, clear dictation, and mastery of legal transcription. These abilities enabled her to pursue professional secretarial work at a time when administrative roles required rigorous technical proficiency and attention to complex statutory documentation.

Marriage to Charles Harrelson and Raising Three Sons as a Single Mother

In 1959, Diane married Charles Voyde Harrelson, a charismatic salesman from Texas. The couple initially settled in Midland, Texas, welcoming three sons in rapid succession: Woodrow Tracy "Woody" Harrelson (born July 1961), followed by Brett and Jordan. However, Charles Harrelson harbored a dangerous double life involving illicit gambling, organized crime connections, and eventual contract killings, which led to his frequent disappearances from the household.

In 1968, recognizing that her husband's criminal lifestyle posed an imminent danger to her children's emotional and physical safety, Diane made the brave and definitive decision to divorce Charles. Packing what few possessions they owned, she relocated her three young sons back to her native hometown of Lebanon, Ohio, where her parents and extended family could offer modest support.

Similar to the family journeys chronicled in our studies of Doug Robbie and dedicated community leaders, Diane stepped forward as the sole provider, protector, and guiding light for her three growing boys.

Professional Career as a Legal Secretary and Civic Faith

To keep food on the table and ensure her boys attended school properly clothed, Diane Oswald worked tirelessly as a legal secretary in Lebanon and neighboring Dayton, Ohio. Legal secretarial work during the 1970s was demanding, often requiring 50-hour workweeks preparing complex briefs, trial filings, and client correspondence.

Despite arriving home exhausted from long workdays, Diane prioritized creating a loving, intellectually stimulating atmosphere. She read extensively with her sons, encouraged public speaking and drama classes, and ensured they were actively involved in church youth programs. Her disciplined ethical foundation helped shield Woody, Brett, and Jordan from the shadow of their father's later high-profile trials and life imprisonment.

Diane Lou Oswald Net Worth and Financial Security

Throughout her career as a legal secretary, Diane Lou Oswald lived frugally, accumulating a modest personal savings portfolio estimated at approximately $1 Million. Following Woody Harrelson's rise to international stardom in the mid-1980s, his first priority was ensuring his mother never had to worry about financial security again.

Woody, whose career earnings have accumulated a net worth exceeding $70 Million, purchased a comfortable home for his mother, provided comprehensive healthcare resources, and regularly hosted her on film sets around the world. Diane's golden years have been characterized by peace, security, and the boundless gratitude of her three sons.

Life Today and Relationship with Her Sons

Today, at 89 years of age, Diane Lou Oswald enjoys a tranquil, cherished retirement surrounded by her children, grandchildren, and lifelong friends. While Woody resides primarily on a solar-powered organic estate in Maui, Hawaii, he maintains constant communication with Diane and frequently reunites with her for family holidays.

Diane's story is a shining testament to the transformative power of single mothers who refuse to let adverse circumstances extinguish their children's potential. Her steady moral compass helped shape one of cinema's most genuine and beloved artists.
