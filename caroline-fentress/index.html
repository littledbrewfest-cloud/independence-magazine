Caroline Fentress: Inside Her Life With Chris O'Donnell, Her Age, Career, and Five Children

A comprehensive biographical look at Caroline Fentress: her background as an educator, 29-year marriage to actor Chris O'Donnell, family life with five children, and private pursuits.

By Sarah Jenkins
Senior Lifestyle & Culture Writer
9 min read October 21, 2026

In an entertainment capital celebrated for volatile romances and rapid divorces, the nearly three-decade union between veteran actor Chris O’Donnell and his wife, Caroline Fentress, stands as an extraordinary model of stability. Long before O’Donnell completed his celebrated fourteen-season run as Special Agent G. Callen on CBS’s NCIS: Los Angeles, or took flight as Robin in Hollywood blockbusters, he made a life-altering commitment to a dedicated elementary school teacher who possessed zero desire for personal celebrity. Together since the early 1990s and married since 1997, the couple has quietly raised five children in Southern California while protecting their household from invasive public scrutiny.

Unlike many celebrity spouses who leverage high-profile partnerships into lifestyle influencer careers or unscripted television deals, Caroline Fentress has consistently chosen a private, purpose-driven path. Her grounded values, Midwestern warmth, and professional background in childhood education have made her the emotional backbone of the O’Donnell household through every chapter of her husband’s evolving career.

Who Is Caroline Fentress?

Caroline Fentress is an American educator, community philanthropist, and devoted mother recognized primarily as the longtime wife of Hollywood actor Chris O’Donnell. Born in Minnesota and educated in Florida, Fentress spent her early adult years teaching young children in primary classrooms, developing pedagogical expertise and patience that would later shape her parenting philosophy.

While millions of television viewers recognize Chris O’Donnell from iconic roles in Scent of a Woman, Circle of Friends, and his decade-plus tenure on CBS's procedural drama, industry insiders consistently point to Caroline as the anchor that allowed O’Donnell to navigate peak stardom without losing his personal bearings. Similar to other grounded Hollywood marriages covered on our publication—including Cathleen Oveson and Kenny Johnson, or Kelly Tisdale and Mike Myers—Caroline and Chris built an enduring family life by deliberately separating work from home.

Early Life, Education, and Exact Age in 2026

Caroline Fentress was born on January 3, 1973, in Minneapolis, Minnesota. As of 2026, Caroline is 53 years old. Raised in a close-knit Midwestern family that placed strong emphasis on educational discipline, community service, and traditional family cohesion, she grew up appreciating quiet suburban stability over metropolitan flash.

Following high school graduation, Caroline enrolled at Rollins College, a prestigious private liberal arts institution in Winter Park, Florida. There, she earned a Bachelor of Arts degree in Elementary Education. Her college years reinforced her love for childhood literacy and classroom development, preparing her for several years of fulfilling professional work in primary education before family responsibilities became her foremost priority.

Career in Education Prior to Family Life

Before stepping into the Hollywood social calendar as Chris O'Donnell's partner, Caroline Fentress established her own professional identity as an elementary school teacher. She taught primary school children in both Washington, D.C., and Los Angeles, earning praise from school administrators and parents for her compassionate approach, structured curriculum, and ability to foster inclusive classroom environments.

Colleagues noted that even when Chris O’Donnell’s Hollywood profile surged following his Golden Globe-nominated role opposite Al Pacino in Scent of a Woman, Caroline remained entirely dedicated to her classroom duties. Her background in developmental psychology and early childhood instruction ultimately served as an invaluable foundation when she and Chris welcomed five children across an eight-year span.

How Caroline Fentress Met Chris O'Donnell

The romantic story of Caroline Fentress and Chris O’Donnell began in the early 1990s through family connections rather than Hollywood casting agencies. Chris was sharing an apartment in Los Angeles with Caroline’s brother, Andrew Fentress. During a casual weekend visit to see her brother, Caroline crossed paths with Chris for the first time.

O’Donnell has often recounted in interviews that he was instantly captivated by Caroline’s warm demeanor, natural laugh, and total lack of pretension. Though Chris was rapidly becoming one of the most sought-after young leading men in America, Caroline treated him simply as her brother’s affable roommate. That grounded dynamic sparked an immediate, authentic connection that evolved into a devoted courtship lasting three years.

Their 1997 Wedding and Core Family Values

On April 19, 1997, Caroline Fentress and Chris O’Donnell were married in a traditional Catholic ceremony at St. Patrick’s Church in Washington, D.C. Surrounded by close family, childhood friends, and select industry peers, the wedding was an intimate, elegant celebration focused on solemn commitment rather than celebrity extravagance.

At the time of their marriage, Chris was just 26 years old and in high demand globally following Batman Forever. Many studio executives and publicists cautioned young leading actors against early marriage, claiming it could compromise their marketable bachelor image. O’Donnell rejected that advice outright, stating that his commitment to Caroline and his dream of building a large, traditional family was infinitely more important than chasing superficial industry perceptions.

Raising Five Children in Southern California

Between 1999 and 2007, Caroline and Chris expanded their family with the birth of five children. Each child brought new vitality to their household, turning their Pacific Palisades residence into a bustling center of activity:

  • Lily Anne O’Donnell: Born on September 3, 1999 (age 27 in 2026).
  • Christopher Eugene O’Donnell Jr.: Born on October 24, 2000 (age 26 in 2026).
  • Charles McHugh O’Donnell: Born on July 11, 2003 (age 23 in 2026).
  • Finley O’Donnell: Born on March 24, 2006 (age 20 in 2026).
  • Maeve Frances O’Donnell: Born on December 10, 2007 (age 18 in 2026).

Raising five children while managing a rigorous television filming schedule required extraordinary organizational diligence from Caroline. Chris worked long twelve-hour production days filming NCIS: Los Angeles on Paramount soundstages, but Caroline ensured that family dinner rituals, school sports schedules, parent-teacher conferences, and weekend church visits were strictly maintained. Several of their children made brief, playful cameo appearances on NCIS: Los Angeles during its run, creating cherished family souvenirs.

The Secret Behind Their Nearly Three-Decade Marriage

When asked by media interviewers how they have preserved a happy, stable marriage for nearly thirty years, both Caroline and Chris attribute their success to shared Midwestern roots, mutual compromise, and emotional boundaries. They have never permitted reality television cameras into their private residence, never aired personal disagreements in public, and consciously cultivate relationships with non-celebrity neighborhood families.

Chris has frequently praised Caroline as an extraordinary wife and mother, noting that her emotional intuition and quiet wisdom prevented the superficial pressures of Hollywood from penetrating their front door. For her part, Caroline has accompanied Chris to major industry milestones—such as his 2015 Hollywood Walk of Fame star dedication ceremony—with graceful poise and quiet pride.

Caroline Fentress’s Net Worth and Family Assets

As of 2026, Caroline Fentress holds an estimated independent net worth of approximately $1.5 Million, reflecting her personal assets, educational consulting engagements, and community foundation investments. When combined with Chris O'Donnell's entertainment earnings, the family’s total wealth exceeds $25 Million.

O’Donnell’s sustained financial strength stems from his long-running starring contract on NCIS: Los Angeles, where he commanded top-tier episodic salaries alongside lucrative international syndication royalties. The couple has invested prudently in premium Southern California real estate, maintaining an elegant, private compound in Los Angeles that serves as the central gathering place for their adult children and extended family.

What Is Caroline Fentress Doing Today?

Today, at 53 years old, Caroline Fentress continues to lead an active, fulfilling life in California. With their younger children transitioning into university life and young adulthood, Caroline devotes increased time to local educational non-profits, youth literacy initiatives, and coastal wellness activities.

She remains a beloved figure within her community and a celebrated example of how personal authenticity, marital dedication, and strong family values can flourish quietly alongside one of television's most visible stars.
