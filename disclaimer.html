Compliance Statement • Updated October 2026

General Informational Purpose

All content published on Independence Magazine (independencemagazine.com) is created for journalistic, biographical, educational, and general reading purposes only. It should not be construed as legal, investment, financial, tax, or medical advice.

1. Financial & Investment Non-Advisory Notice

Articles across our Business, Finance, and Crypto desks explore economic trends, corporate dealmaking, valuation estimates, and decentralized protocols. None of this reporting constitutes financial advice or an endorsement to purchase, hold, or divest from any security, equity, commodity, or cryptocurrency asset.

  • Financial markets, venture equity, and digital assets carry substantial capital risk.
  • Past performance referenced in company profiles is no guarantee of future returns.
  • Always consult a certified independent financial advisor (IFA) or registered investment professional prior to committing capital.

2. Health, Medical & Clinical Research Notice

Reporting within our Health and Science desks covers peer-reviewed clinical studies, oncology algorithms, epigenetic breakthroughs, and biomedical technology. This material is designed to inform public understanding of scientific progress:

  • It is never a substitute for direct diagnosis, personalized medical advice, or treatment by qualified medical practitioners.
  • Never disregard professional medical guidance or delay seeking care based on articles read on Independence Magazine.

3. Advertising, Programmatic Networks & Sponsorships

In accordance with Federal Trade Commission (FTC) guidelines and Google Publisher policies:

  • Programmatic Advertising: We may display automated programmatic advertisements served through third-party networks, including Google AdSense. These advertisements are clearly demarcated from editorial content.
  • No Hidden Sponsorships: We do not publish paid promotional content disguised as independent reporting. Any paid partner feature is clearly marked with an explicit disclosure tag.
  • Affiliate Links: If an editorial article includes an affiliate tracking link, this will be stated explicitly. We do not alter our journalistic assessment to accommodate affiliate partnerships.

4. Biographical Estimates & Net Worth Reporting

Biographical articles may contain estimates of corporate valuations, wealth holdings, or earnings for prominent public figures. While our reporters cross-reference these estimates with regulatory disclosures, real estate deeds, and financial audits, these figures represent analytical approximations unless explicitly affirmed by public audited documentation.

5. External Links Disclaimer

Independence Magazine articles frequently link to external primary sources, government whitepapers, and archival databases. We do not endorse or assume liability for the privacy policies, accuracy, or operational security of external third-party websites.
