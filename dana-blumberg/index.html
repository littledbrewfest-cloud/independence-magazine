Who Is Dr. Dana Blumberg? The Life of Robert Kraft’s Wife, Her Age, Medical Career, and Marriage

An in-depth profile of renowned ophthalmologist Dr. Dana Blumberg: her prestigious medical career at Columbia University, high-profile marriage to Patriots owner Robert Kraft, and philanthropic leadership.

By Marcus Vance
Senior Cultural Affairs Editor
9 min read October 22, 2026

When New England Patriots owner and billionaire industrialist Robert Kraft hosted a surprise wedding celebration in lower Manhattan in October 2022, the entertainment and business worlds paused to celebrate one of the most unexpected, high-profile unions in modern American philanthropy. The woman at Kraft’s side was not a Hollywood starlet or reality media personality, but Dr. Dana Melissa Blumberg—an internationally respected, board-certified ophthalmologist, Columbia University associate professor, and clinical scientist dedicated to eradicating preventable blindness.

Long before her marriage introduced her to NFL owner meetings, Met Gala red carpets, and State Dinners at the White House, Dr. Blumberg had established an impeccable independent reputation across academic medicine. Her groundbreaking clinical research in glaucoma screening and patient-centered eye care earned her prestigious national grants and peer recognition, demonstrating that intellectual substance and high-society visibility can coexist seamlessly.

Who Is Dr. Dana Blumberg?

Dr. Dana Blumberg is an American medical doctor, academic researcher, and health advocate who serves as an associate professor of ophthalmology at Columbia University Medical Center in New York. A specialist in glaucoma diagnosis and surgical intervention, Dr. Blumberg has authored dozens of peer-reviewed scientific studies published in prestigious medical journals.

While she stepped into global public visibility following her relationship and marriage to New England Patriots owner Robert Kraft, Dr. Blumberg remains fundamentally rooted in clinical medicine and public health research. Like other accomplished spouses profiled on our platform—such as Caroline Fentress in education or Kelly Tisdale in culinary entrepreneurship—Dr. Blumberg maintained her own distinguished professional identity long before high-profile matrimony.

Early Life, Education, and Real Age in 2026

Dr. Dana Melissa Blumberg was born in 1974 in New York. As of 2026, she is 52 years old. Raised in an intellectually rigorous family that valued academic pursuit and community health, she exhibited an early aptitude for biological sciences and analytical problem solving.

After graduating from Brown University with high honors, she pursued medical training at the Saint Louis University School of Medicine, where she distinguished herself by simultaneously earning both a Doctor of Medicine (MD) and a Master of Public Health (MPH). She completed her ophthalmology residency at Case Western Reserve University in Cleveland, followed by an elite fellowship in clinical glaucoma care at the prestigious Wilmer Eye Institute at Johns Hopkins University in Baltimore.

An Accomplished Medical Career in Ophthalmology and Glaucoma Research

Joining the faculty at Columbia University’s Department of Ophthalmology, Dr. Blumberg quickly emerged as a national authority in glaucoma detection. Her research has focused heavily on reducing disparities in vision healthcare, designing innovative mobile screening technologies to catch optic nerve damage in underserved populations before permanent vision loss occurs.

In recognition of her scientific contributions, she was awarded research funding from the National Institutes of Health (NIH) and received the 2009 American Glaucoma Society Clinician Scientist Award. In 2013, the Wilmer Eye Institute recognized her with the prestigious Li-Baer Award. Patients and academic colleagues frequently describe Dr. Blumberg as an empathetic, meticulous physician who combines world-class surgical precision with deep bedside compassion.

How Dr. Dana Blumberg and Robert Kraft Met

The relationship between Dr. Blumberg and Robert Kraft began in 2019 through mutual philanthropic and healthcare networks in the northeastern United States. Kraft, whose beloved first wife Myra Kraft had tragically passed away from cancer in 2011, found deep solace and renewed joy in Dr. Blumberg’s warmth, sharp intellect, and shared commitment to humanitarian causes.

The couple made their initial public appearances together in late 2019, attending an Elton John AIDS Foundation event and select New England Patriots games at Gillette Stadium. In early 2022, Kraft proposed with a breathtaking multi-carat diamond ring, which Dr. Blumberg was seen wearing at the NFL Honors gala in Los Angeles.

Their Star-Studded 2022 Surprise Wedding in New York

On October 14, 2022, Robert Kraft and Dr. Dana Blumberg married in an unforgettable surprise ceremony at the Hall des Lumières in downtown Manhattan. Guests, who initially believed they were attending a casual celebratory dinner party titled "Kickoff and a Touchdown," were stunned when Kraft and Blumberg took the stage for their vows.

The guest list included over 250 of America’s most prominent figures in sports, business, and entertainment: Tom Brady, Jon Bon Jovi, Ed Sheeran, Meek Mill, NFL Commissioner Roger Goodell, and Sir Elton John, who flew in specially to perform a private set for the newlyweds. Despite the immense public stature of the attendees, guests noted that the ceremony felt deeply emotional, spiritual, and sincere.

Navigating the Public Eye: From NFL Sidelines to the Met Gala

Following their marriage, Dr. Blumberg embraced her public role with elegance and discretion. She accompanies Kraft to major cultural and philanthropic functions—including the Met Gala, the Foundation to Combat Antisemitism summits, and State Dinners at the White House—while maintaining her active medical license and professional affiliations.

Despite their thirty-three-year age difference, close associates consistently emphasize that their connection is rooted in intellectual equality, mutual admiration, and joyful companionship. Dr. Blumberg brings energy and clinical focus to Kraft’s extensive healthcare philanthropy, advising on strategic medical grants and hospital initiatives across New England and New York.

Dr. Dana Blumberg’s Net Worth and Charitable Leadership

As of 2026, Dr. Dana Blumberg’s independent net worth is estimated at approximately $5 Million, accumulated through her twenty-year career as a senior surgeon, university professor, medical consultant, and clinical researcher. When combined with Robert Kraft’s fortune—which encompasses The Kraft Group, the New England Patriots, New England Revolution, Gillette Stadium, and vast commercial holdings—their combined household fortune exceeds $11 Billion.

Dr. Blumberg plays an integral role in directing the couple’s vast charitable distributions, supporting pediatric oncology programs, vision research foundations, and educational access initiatives for underprivileged youth.

What Is Dr. Dana Blumberg Doing Today?

Today, at 52 years old, Dr. Dana Blumberg balances private family life in Massachusetts and New York with healthcare philanthropy and medical advisory roles. She remains an inspiring figure who demonstrated that a woman can achieve pinnacle success in clinical science while gracefully embracing one of the most visible public partnerships in American sports.
