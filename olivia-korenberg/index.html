Olivia Korenberg: Her Interior Design Career, Marriage to Seann William Scott, and Post-Divorce Life

An editorial deep dive into California interior designer Olivia Korenberg: founding TwoFold LA, her private four-year marriage to American Pie star Seann William Scott, co-parenting Frankie Rose, and her current design projects.

By Sarah Jenkins
Senior Lifestyle & Culture Writer
9 min read October 26, 2026

In the high-energy intersection where Hollywood notoriety meets architectural artistry, Olivia Korenberg has established an identity distinguished by understated elegance and creative discipline. While global entertainment outlets widely recognized her as the former wife of versatile comedic and dramatic actor Seann William Scott—celebrated worldwide for his iconic portrayal of Steve Stifler in the American Pie franchise—Korenberg was already an accomplished interior architectural designer and creative entrepreneur in Southern California long before her public romance. Her journey underscores the poise of a modern creative who navigates public attention with grace while maintaining unwavering dedication to her craft and young daughter.

Much like other visionary women who established robust design careers alongside notable entertainment figures, including Laura Quinn Hawk building her boutique design studio or Ashton Meem leading executive advertising accounts, Olivia Korenberg represents professional autonomy and contemporary creative excellence.

California Roots, Education, and Early Artistic Foundations

Olivia Korenberg was born in December 1987 in California, growing up in an environment that celebrated academic rigor and visual expression. From her youth, she possessed a refined eye for proportion, spatial harmony, and organic materiality. Following her high school graduation, Korenberg enrolled at the prestigious University of Southern California (USC), earning a Bachelor of Arts in French literature and culture in 2010.

Her immersive study of French aesthetics and European architectural traditions provided a sophisticated philosophical foundation for her subsequent career. Recognizing that interior spaces profoundly influence human emotion and productivity, she continued her education at the University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA), completing an intensive Master’s-level certification in Interior Architecture. This formal training equipped Korenberg with technical proficiency in structural drafting, material sourcing, environmental lighting, and bespoke furniture design.

Launching TwoFold LA: A Distinctive Architectural and Design Vision

In 2011, Korenberg partnered with her close colleague Jenn Pablo to co-found TwoFold LA, a boutique interior design and event production firm based in Los Angeles. At a time when minimalist industrial trends dominated California commercial spaces, TwoFold LA distinguished itself by harmonizing clean, functional lines with vintage textures, rich natural woods, and artisanal ceramics.

Under Korenberg’s co-direction as principal designer, the firm quickly gained traction among upscale residential homeowners and commercial clients across Venice Beach, Santa Monica, and Beverly Hills. Architectural and home decor publications, including Domino, Lonny, and Architectural Digest, praised TwoFold LA for creating environments that felt simultaneously polished and lived-in. Although Korenberg eventually stepped back from day-to-day administrative operations in late 2019 to transition toward independent design consulting, her foundational imprint remains highly respected across California’s design industry.

Relationship and Quiet 2019 Wedding with Seann William Scott

In mid-2018, Olivia Korenberg met actor Seann William Scott through mutual friends in Los Angeles. Known throughout his career for fiercely guarding his private life from red carpet circus culture, Scott found in Korenberg an intellectually stimulating partner whose creativity was rooted in tangible art rather than industry self-promotion.

In September 2019, during press interviews for his film Already Gone, Scott casually mentioned to reporters that he had recently fallen in love and married a wonderful woman who was not in the entertainment business, affectionately describing her as 'just an incredible human.' A few weeks later, public records confirmed that the couple had exchanged vows on September 21, 2019, in an intimate, private gathering attended only by immediate family and closest friends.

Welcoming Daughter Frankie Rose and Modern Motherhood

In June 2020, amidst the quietude of the global pandemic, Korenberg and Scott welcomed their first child, a daughter named Frankie Rose Scott. The birth of Frankie Rose brought immense joy to the couple’s household, and both parents committed themselves wholeheartedly to hands-on parenting.

Scott often shared in subsequent interviews how fatherhood fundamentally reorganized his life priorities, noting that every career decision was evaluated through the lens of providing a peaceful, loving home for his daughter. Korenberg balanced motherhood with select bespoke design projects, intentionally moderating her client roster so she could be present for Frankie Rose’s early developmental years.

Navigating Separation and Amicable Co-Parenting in 2024–2026

In February 2024, after more than four years of marriage, Seann William Scott filed for legal dissolution of marriage in Los Angeles Superior Court, citing irreconcilable differences. According to court filings, the couple had quietly separated several months earlier, in October 2023.

Unlike contentious Hollywood divorces that devolve into public litigation, Korenberg and Scott handled their separation with exemplary maturity. Scott requested joint legal and physical custody of Frankie Rose, and both parties entered into amicable private negotiations regarding property division and child support. Sources close to the former couple consistently note that in 2026, both Olivia and Seann maintain a respectful, cooperative co-parenting partnership, ensuring that their six-year-old daughter experiences consistent love, stability, and mutual parental presence.

Interior Architecture, Event Curation, and Boutique Consulting

Following her separation, Olivia Korenberg refocused her energy on her primary passion: bespoke interior architecture and creative consulting. Working independently with a select roster of private homeowners and boutique commercial spaces, Korenberg specializes in sustainable residential renovations, spatial layout optimization, and sourcing rare mid-century furniture pieces.

Her design methodology continues to be informed by her European cultural background, blending classical French symmetry with airy, sun-drenched California minimalism. In design circles, clients laud her ability to transform challenging floor plans into calm, cohesive living spaces that reflect the homeowners' authentic personal histories.

Financial Profile and Estimated Net Worth in 2026

As of 2026, financial research analysts estimate Olivia Korenberg’s independent net worth at between $600,000 and $1.0 Million USD. This valuation reflects more than fifteen years of active professional practice in high-end Los Angeles interior design, design consultation fees, profits realized from TwoFold LA, and equitable financial settlements finalized during her divorce proceedings.

While Seann William Scott possesses an estimated fortune of approximately $25 Million USD resulting from his long-running film career, Korenberg has consistently supported herself through her thriving design ventures, demonstrating the financial resilience and entrepreneurial acumen that defined her long before her marriage.

Life and Design Directions in 2026

Now in 2026, at 39 years of age, Olivia Korenberg embodies creative maturity and intentional living. Residing in a beautifully appointed home in Los Angeles, she spends her days overseeing custom residential build-outs, visiting artisan craft workshops, and nurturing her daughter Frankie Rose.

Korenberg deliberately avoids personal social media sensationalism, opting instead for a discreet, fulfilling life grounded in authentic relationships and enduring craftsmanship. Her story illustrates that personal fulfillment is defined not by temporary celebrity proximity, but by the lasting beauty one creates through dedicated artistry and loving parenthood.
