Who Is Doug Robbie? Inside the Life, Career, and Story of Margot Robbie's Father

From Dalby sugarcane farming and Queensland real estate development to his complex family dynamic with Hollywood star Margot Robbie.

By Marcus Vance
Investigative Features & Senior Editor
8 min read October 12, 2026

Quick Facts: Doug Robbie

Full NameDoug Robbie
OriginDalby / Gold Coast Hinterland, Queensland, Australia
NationalityAustralian
ProfessionSugarcane Farmer, Agricultural Contractor, Real Estate Developer
Former SpouseSarie Kessler (divorced mid-1990s)
ChildrenAnya Robbie, Lachlan Robbie, Margot Robbie (b. 1990), Cameron Robbie

Margot Robbie stands today as one of the most bankable actresses and prolific producers in global cinema, with blockbuster triumphs ranging from The Wolf of Wall Street to Barbie and multiple Academy Award nominations. In almost every major profile, Robbie openly credits her single mother, physiotherapist Sarie Kessler, for working tirelessly to raise four children on a modest farm in the Gold Coast hinterland. Yet behind the actress's celebrated family story lies a figure who has remained largely enigmatic: her father, Doug Robbie.

A former sugarcane farming magnate and commercial agricultural contractor who later transitioned into Queensland residential real estate, Doug Robbie is an intriguing figure whose relationship with his famous daughter has generated extensive international curiosity. Far from the glamorous red carpets of Los Angeles and London, Doug Robbie's life remains rooted in the rugged commercial landscape of rural Australia.

Rural Queensland Roots and the Sugarcane Industry

Doug Robbie's origins trace back to the agricultural heartland of regional Queensland, Australia. Raised in communities where farming, livestock management, and heavy machinery were fundamental parts of daily life, Robbie established himself in the sugarcane harvesting sector in the late 1970s and 1980s.

Sugarcane cultivation in tropical and subtropical Queensland is a demanding, capital-intensive industry requiring meticulous harvest scheduling, transportation logistics, and commercial milling contracts. Rather than remaining a small-scale family farmer, Robbie developed a reputation as a shrewd contractor. He invested in industrial cutting equipment and leased agricultural parcels, coordinating large-scale harvesting operations across the Darling Downs and coastal Queensland farming corridors.

Marriage to Sarie Kessler and the Dalby Household

In the 1980s, Doug Robbie married Sarie Kessler, a dedicated physiotherapist. The couple settled in the regional agricultural hub of Dalby, west of Brisbane, where they began raising their family. Over the course of their marriage, they welcomed four children: eldest daughter Anya, sons Lachlan and Cameron, and daughter Margot Elise Robbie, born on July 2, 1990.

While Robbie's commercial contracting ventures yielded significant financial returns, the demanding nature of his business and differing personal values created strain within the household. Much like the early domestic pressures faced by Kathryn Burrhus during collegiate athletic relocations, the demands of commercial enterprise often created geographical and emotional separation within the young Robbie home.

The Separation: Growing Up on the Kessler Family Farm

When Margot was approximately five years old in the mid-1990s, Doug Robbie and Sarie Kessler separated, eventually finalizing their divorce. Following the separation, Sarie took primary physical custody of the four children, relocating to the Gold Coast hinterland near Currubin Valley.

Sarie raised the children largely as a single parent, balancing her work as a clinical physiotherapist with domestic responsibilities. Margot and her siblings spent their formative years outdoors on their grandparents' property, hunting wild boar, surfing along the Gold Coast beaches, and learning self-reliance. In interviews with Vogue and Vanity Fair, Margot has recounted how she worked three jobs simultaneously as a teenager—making sandwiches at Subway, cleaning surf houses, and working retail—to assist her mother with household expenses.

Margot Robbie's Meteoric Rise to Hollywood Superstardom

Margot Robbie's determination to achieve financial independence was clear from early adolescence. At seventeen, she moved independently to Melbourne, landing a breakout role as Donna Freedman on the iconic Australian soap opera Neighbours. By 2011, she relocated to the United States, quickly landing the lead in ABC's Pan Am before her iconic role opposite Leonardo DiCaprio in Martin Scorsese's The Wolf of Wall Street (2013).

Robbie's loyalty to her mother became legendary in 2014 when, using her initial Hollywood paychecks, she quietly paid off the entirety of Sarie Kessler's home mortgage for her 60th birthday. The gesture symbolized the deep gratitude she felt for the parent who had sacrificed everything for her education and acting lessons.

Transition into Real Estate and Commercial Land Development

Following his years in sugarcane contracting, Doug Robbie diversified his capital into property investment. Queensland experienced rapid suburban expansion in the 2000s and 2010s, with agricultural acreage near Brisbane and the Gold Coast hinterland rezoned for residential subdivisions.

Robbie acquired parcels of land, participating in commercial subdivisions and industrial equipment leasing. Public property records in Queensland indicate his involvement in several high-value property transactions, cementing his financial stability independently of his children's careers. Friends in regional Queensland describe him as a sharp negotiator who understands land valuation, water rights, and developmental zoning.

The Family Dynamic: Distant Yet Respectful

Over the years, Australian and international tabloids have occasionally sensationalized the dynamic between Doug Robbie and his children. When asked by journalists in 2016 about her father, Margot offered a candid, measured response, stating that while they were not close, there was no active malice, remarking: "He's not really a part of my life... but there's no bad blood."

However, accounts from extended family members indicate that Doug Robbie has maintained occasional contact with his sons, Lachlan and Cameron. Cameron Robbie, who has also pursued acting in Australia, has occasionally shared moments with his father on social media. Doug has respected his daughter's privacy, turning down lucrative tabloid interview offers and maintaining complete discretion regarding her Hollywood career.

Life Today and Estimated Net Worth

Today, Doug Robbie resides in regional Queensland, enjoying a comfortable, semi-retired lifestyle focused on private investments, golf, and regional community events. He has never attempted to leverage Margot's immense international celebrity for commercial publicity or personal fame.

His estimated net worth is valued between $5 Million and $8 Million AUD, driven by decades of sugarcane contracting revenues, agricultural equipment sales, and residential real estate assets in Queensland. While his relationship with Hollywood's leading star remains complex, his independent commercial success in regional Australia stands on its own merits.
