Rouba Saadeh: Age, Net Worth, Career, Marriage to Michele Morrone, and Life Today

Discover the life, career, and family of Lebanese fashion designer Rouba Saadeh, her work with Elie Saab, and her relationship with Italian actor Michele Morrone.

By Sarah Jenkins
Senior Biographical Correspondent
8 min read October 14, 2026

Rouba Saadeh is a talented Lebanese fashion designer, creative director, and graphic coordinator who has built an impressive international career in luxury fashion. While she first gained global public attention through her marriage to Italian actor and singer Michele Morrone—the breakout star of Netflix's hit film 365 Days—Saadeh had already established her own distinct reputation in haute couture through her high-level creative work with luxury fashion house Elie Saab.

Today, Rouba Saadeh continues to thrive as an independent creative professional and devoted mother of two sons. Her balance of high-end design expertise, multicultural business experience, and graceful co-parenting has made her an admired figure in the Mediterranean and Middle Eastern creative communities.

Who Is Rouba Saadeh?

Rouba Saadeh is a Lebanese-born fashion executive and designer based between Beirut, Milan, and Dubai. Fluent in Arabic, English, French, and Italian, she combines a strong academic background in graphic arts with specialized training in luxury brand management. Over the past fifteen years, she has worked across garment construction, visual marketing, high-fashion styling, and retail concept development.

Early Life in Beirut, Lebanon and Age

Rouba Saadeh was born on April 15, 1987, in Beirut, Lebanon. Growing up in a cosmopolitan and culturally vibrant environment, she developed an early fascination with architecture, Mediterranean textiles, and visual illustration. As of 2026, Rouba Saadeh is 39 years old, celebrating her birthday under the Aries zodiac sign.

Supported by a close-knit family that encouraged higher education, Saadeh dedicated her teenage years to fine arts and language studies. Her upbringing in Lebanon gave her a unique appreciation for classic craftsmanship and cosmopolitan aesthetic traditions, which later became hallmarks of her design work.

Educational Background and Design Training

After finishing high school in Beirut, Saadeh enrolled at the prestigious Lebanese American University (LAU), where she earned her Bachelor of Science degree in Graphic Design in 2008. Eager to expand her formal knowledge of apparel design and commercial textiles, she subsequently earned a Post Graduate Diploma in Fashion Design from the renowned Istituto Marangoni in Milan, Italy—one of Europe's top fashion academies.

To further sharpen her executive skills, she completed a Master's degree in Fashion and Luxury Brand Management at the European Institute of Design (IED). This rigorous combination of technical graphic design and luxury commerce positioned her for senior roles in global fashion houses.

Career in Fashion and Work With Elie Saab

Rouba Saadeh began her professional career working as a graphic designer for Alfa MIC in Beirut before transitioning full-time into the fashion industry. Her breakthrough came when she joined world-renowned haute couture designer Elie Saab as a Senior Coordinator and Assistant to the Creative Director.

At Elie Saab, Saadeh played a hands-on role in coordinating seasonal haute couture collections, supervising pattern realization, managing runway showroom presentations in Paris, and directing visual communication assets. Her sharp eye for balance and fabric drape earned high praise from design colleagues across Beirut and Paris.

Her Concept Store: Les Paradis Des Fous

In 2013, driven by an entrepreneurial vision, Saadeh launched her own designer-based concept store named Les Paradis Des Fous. The project was conceived as a curated artistic collective, bringing together independent talent from Lebanon, Italy, and France to showcase bespoke apparel, artisan jewelry, and contemporary lifestyle items.

Although she later paused the physical store operations to focus on family life and her senior corporate roles with Elie Saab, the initiative demonstrated her forward-thinking ability to curate distinctive lifestyle brands outside mainstream commercial channels.

Relationship and Marriage to Michele Morrone

In 2011, Rouba Saadeh met Italian actor and singer Michele Morrone through mutual acquaintances while Morrone was visiting Lebanon. The two shared an immediate emotional and artistic bond, navigating a long-distance relationship between Beirut and Rome before tying the knot in 2014.

Their wedding was an intimate civil ceremony celebrated with close family and friends in both Italy and Lebanon. During the early years of their marriage, Michele was working diligently as an aspiring actor in Italian television and theater, while Rouba provided steady emotional and professional support. Her focus on family and creative independence parallels other accomplished spouses like Malaak Compton and Mia Weier, who maintained their own distinct careers outside the celebrity glare.

Divorce, Co-Parenting, and Life After Michele Morrone

After four years of marriage, Rouba Saadeh and Michele Morrone mutually decided to divorce in 2018. Crucially, their separation occurred nearly two years before Morrone was cast in 365 Days, which transformed him into an international household name in 2020.

Unlike many high-profile celebrity breakups that devolve into tabloid drama, Saadeh and Morrone handled their split with maturity and mutual respect. In interviews, Morrone has frequently spoken with deep affection about Rouba, describing her as an exceptional mother and one of the most important people in his life. The two continue to co-parent their sons collaboratively, regularly coordinating family holidays and school milestones.

Rouba Saadeh's Children: Marcus and Brando

Rouba Saadeh is the proud mother of two sons. Her firstborn, Marcus Morrone, was born in 2014, and her second son, Brando Morrone, arrived in 2017. Both boys are growing up bilingually, spending time between Lebanon and Italy.

Saadeh is deeply dedicated to providing her children with a grounded, well-rounded upbringing away from media sensationalism. Her social media profiles occasionally share warm, candid moments of family outings, beach trips, and artistic activities with her boys.

Rouba Saadeh Net Worth and Financial Standing

As of 2026, Rouba Saadeh's estimated net worth is between $1.5 million and $2 million. Her wealth has been built through her long-term salary as a Senior Coordinator at Elie Saab, independent brand design consulting fees, creative directing engagements, and private commercial investments.

Her financial independence reflects a solid career built on formal educational credentials and executive performance rather than celebrity alimony or public endorsements.

Where Is Rouba Saadeh Today?

Today, Rouba Saadeh resides primarily in Beirut, frequently traveling to Dubai and Milan for corporate fashion consulting and collaborative design projects. She remains an active force in luxury ready-to-wear coordination and brand visual direction.

Outside of work, she enjoys contemporary art exhibitions, Mediterranean coastal travel, and fitness. Her ability to navigate public attention with grace while maintaining a successful corporate career has cemented her status as an inspiring modern professional.
