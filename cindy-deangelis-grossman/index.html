Who Is Cindy DeAngelis Grossman? The Biography of Herschel Walker's Ex-Wife, Career, and Life Today

From their college days at the University of Georgia to building a career in business and design.

By Marcus Vance
Sports & Public Affairs Biographer

Throughout the 1980s and 1990s, Herschel Walker was one of the most prominent athletes in American sports—a Heisman Trophy winner at the University of Georgia and an accomplished NFL running back. Alongside him during his formative collegiate years and prime professional seasons was his college sweetheart and former wife, Cindy DeAngelis Grossman.

Over a nineteen-year marriage, Grossman managed their household across multiple relocations and built an independent career in interior design and business consulting. Today, she maintains a dignified, private life while remaining closely involved with her family.

Early Life and Collegiate Background

Cindy DeAngelis was born in 1962 in Florida into an Italian-American family. Her father, Thomas DeAngelis, worked as a real estate investor and businessman, giving her early exposure to business management and entrepreneurship.

During her youth, she participated in track and cheerleading before enrolling at the University of Georgia in Athens. There, she pursued studies in business administration and economics, demonstrating strong organizational skills and a keen interest in commercial design.

Meeting Herschel Walker and 1983 Marriage

Cindy first met Herschel Walker in the training facilities at the University of Georgia, where both were recovering from track-related injuries. Their friendship quickly developed into a relationship during Walker's historic Heisman-winning collegiate football career.

On March 31, 1983, following Walker's decision to turn professional with the New Jersey Generals in the USFL, the pair married in a family ceremony. When Walker later transitioned to the NFL with the Dallas Cowboys, Minnesota Vikings, Philadelphia Eagles, and New York Giants, Grossman supported the family through frequent cross-country moves.

Motherhood and Family Life

In 1999, the couple welcomed their son, Christian Walker. Grossman focused significant energy on providing stability and education for their son, encouraging his artistic pursuits and competitive cheerleading achievements.

Christian Walker later achieved national recognition as a cheerleading champion and social media commentator, frequently speaking warmly of his mother's dedication and support during his upbringing.

2002 Separation and Independent Career

After nineteen years of marriage, Cindy and Herschel Walker separated in 2001 and finalized their divorce in 2002. The separation was prompted by personal challenges, which Walker later discussed openly in his 2008 memoir regarding his mental health journey.

Following the divorce, Grossman focused on expanding her independent design consultancy, assisting residential clients with renovations, space planning, and decor in Texas and Florida.

Life Today and Net Worth

Cindy DeAngelis Grossman has largely remained out of the media spotlight, choosing not to participate in partisan politics during Walker's 2022 Senate campaign. She remarried in the mid-2000s and continues to live comfortably in the southern United States.

Her personal net worth is estimated at $1.5 Million to $2 Million, derived from property investments, divorce financial settlements, and her design business activities.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Where did Cindy DeAngelis and Herschel Walker meet?

They met as student-athletes at the University of Georgia in Athens in the early 1980s.

How long were Cindy Grossman and Herschel Walker married?

They were married for 19 years, from March 1983 until their divorce was finalized in 2002.

What is Cindy DeAngelis Grossman's profession?

She has worked primarily in business administration and residential interior design consulting.
