Who Is Deanna Burditt? The Story of Rick Harrison’s Wife, Her Age, Career, and Life Away from Pawn Stars

Discover the private life and professional background of Deanna Burditt, legal secretary and longtime spouse of History Channel star Rick Harrison.

By Marcus Vance
Senior Cultural Affairs Editor
9 min read October 18, 2026

On the bustling stretch of Las Vegas Boulevard, the World Famous Gold & Silver Pawn Shop welcomes thousands of curious visitors every day. As the epicenter of History Channel's juggernaut reality series Pawn Stars, the store and its charismatic co-owner Rick Harrison have been broadcast into more than 500 million households worldwide. Yet away from the glare of television production cameras and high-stakes antique negotiations stands Deanna Burditt—a woman who has consciously chosen privacy, professional dedication, and grounded domestic stability.

Married to Harrison since July 2013, Burditt has earned widespread respect among fans and industry observers alike for steadfastly refusing to become a reality television personality. Rather than seeking influencer status or on-camera screen time, she has maintained her independent career in the legal sector, providing a stabilizing counterbalance to her husband's hectic commercial empire.

Deanna Burditt Age and Early Professional Background

Deanna Burditt was born in 1977 in the United States. Growing up in the American West, she was raised in a family that prioritized education, personal responsibility, and practical vocational skills. As of 2026, Deanna Burditt is 49 years old.

Unlike public figures whose careers unfold in entertainment headlines, Burditt charted a quiet, steady course through business administration. After completing specialized post-secondary coursework in secretarial and paralegal studies, she entered the competitive legal sector in Nevada, establishing herself as an organized, dependable legal secretary supporting civil litigation attorneys and corporate law offices across the Las Vegas valley.

Her early professional years instilled in her a profound appreciation for discretion, detail-oriented research, and emotional composure. Whether coordinating complex case files or liaising with municipal courts in Clark County, Burditt earned a reputation for reliability that made her an invaluable asset to every legal practice she joined.

Career as a Legal Secretary and Executive Assistant

For more than two decades, Burditt has served as an executive legal secretary and administrative specialist. In this demanding role, she manages complex legal documentation, coordinates court calendars, handles discovery filings, and ensures seamless confidential correspondence between attorneys, judges, and corporate clients.

Her colleagues have long praised her professionalism, calm demeanor under tight filing deadlines, and fierce discretion. Even after her marriage to a multi-millionaire reality television figure, Burditt chose to retain her career identity. This fierce commitment to personal independence mirrors the professional trajectories explored in our features on Kelly Tisdale and Rouba Saadeh, demonstrating that high-profile marriages do not require abandoning one's individual craft.

Working in law firms provided Burditt with a distinct perspective on celebrity and contracts. When media interest surged around her engagement to Harrison, she approached the public curiosity with the same calm neutrality she applied to legal briefs, ensuring her private employment remained unaffected by tabloid gossip.

How She Met Rick Harrison and Their 2013 Laguna Beach Wedding

Deanna Burditt and Rick Harrison first crossed paths in 2011 at Aureole, a renowned upscale restaurant located inside the Mandalay Bay Resort in Las Vegas. Introduced through mutual business acquaintances, Harrison was struck by Burditt's warmth, intelligence, and refreshing lack of fascination with his television celebrity. At the time, both had experienced previous marriages and shared a realistic, mature understanding of life's complexities.

After a year of dating, Harrison proposed to Burditt on Valentine's Day in 2012 at the very same restaurant where they first met. On July 21, 2013, the couple tied the knot before 180 close friends and family members at the picturesque Ritz-Carlton, Laguna Niguel in Dana Point, California. The sunset ceremony was infused with touches of rock-and-roll humor: Danny Koker, star of Counting Cars and an ordained minister, officiated the vows, while Austin "Chumlee" Russell served as the playful ring bearer.

Guests marveled at the genuine, joyful atmosphere of the ceremony. Following the oceanfront vows, attendees enjoyed live musical performances, including a special set by the classic rock band Steel Panther, reflecting Rick's lifelong love for 1980s heavy metal and Deanna's affinity for energetic celebration.

Navigating Family Life and a Blended Household of Six Children

When Burditt and Harrison married, they embraced the challenge of uniting two families into a harmonious blended household. Harrison brought three sons from his prior marriages—Corey, Adam, and Jake—while Burditt was the devoted mother of three daughters from past relationships. Harrison frequently joked in media interviews that they were living a modern-day Brady Bunch, with three boys and three girls under one roof.

Burditt took on the role of family coordinator with patience and warmth, fostering close bonds between step-siblings while fiercely safeguarding her daughters' privacy from intrusive media scrutiny. The family faced deep sorrow in early 2024 when Harrison's son Adam tragically passed away, a loss that prompted widespread outpouring of sympathy across the television community. Throughout difficult seasons, Burditt's quiet strength and emotional support have served as the anchor holding the extended Harrison clan together.

Her ability to maintain normalcy during periods of intense grief and public attention has been recognized by close family associates as one of the defining foundations of Rick's personal peace in recent years.

Deanna Burditt Net Worth and Combined Household Finances

Independently, Deanna Burditt has accumulated an estimated net worth of $250,000 to $400,000 through her career as an executive legal secretary and prudent personal savings. Jointly with Rick Harrison—whose business assets include the Gold & Silver Pawn Shop, commercial retail properties, and television production royalties—the household net worth exceeds $9 Million.

The couple resides in a spacious, custom-designed estate in the Summerlin community of Las Vegas and enjoys spending downtime at Harrison's rural ranch in Oregon. Despite their comfortable wealth, friends and neighbors describe Burditt as remarkably down-to-earth, focused on family dinners, camping trips, and community charity work rather than ostentatious displays of luxury.

Why She Chose Privacy Away from Reality Television Cameras

In an era dominated by social media micro-celebrity, Burditt's decision to stay off television cameras is both rare and deliberate. Producers on Pawn Stars have occasionally extended invitations for cameo appearances or family specials, but Burditt has consistently declined, explaining that she already has a career she loves and zero desire for public recognition.

Her conscious choice has helped preserve a normal, drama-free domestic sanctuary for her family. By keeping home life distinct from television performance, Deanna Burditt has built an enduring partnership that stands resilient against the pressures of celebrity culture.
