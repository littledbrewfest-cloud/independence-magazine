Alex Cowper-Smith: Age, Net Worth, Goldman Sachs Career, Marriage to Alice Eve, and Life Today

Learn about British financier Alex Cowper-Smith: his age, Goldman Sachs investment banking career, marriage to actress Alice Eve, and life today.

By Sarah Jenkins
Senior Biographical Correspondent
8 min read October 15, 2026

Alexander Michael Cowper-Smith is a distinguished British corporate financier, investment banker, and institutional advisor best known in mainstream public spheres as the former husband of celebrated British-American actress Alice Eve. While Eve achieved international acclaim through starring roles in blockbuster films like Star Trek Into Darkness, She's Out of My League, and Men in Black 3, Cowper-Smith established a formidable reputation of his own in the rigorous corridors of European high finance, most notably through his senior work at Goldman Sachs.

Their romantic history captivated global media because it represented a genuine childhood sweetheart reunion: the pair first dated as teenagers attending one of London's oldest boarding schools before reconnecting decades later at the height of their respective careers. Today, Alex Cowper-Smith continues to maintain a private, distinguished career in corporate advisory, personifying the classic British tradition of quiet professionalism.

Who Is Alex Cowper-Smith?

Alex Cowper-Smith is a London-based corporate finance professional who specialized in investment banking, capital markets structuring, and equity financing. Known for his analytical acumen and discreet professional demeanor, he operated at elite tiers of global banking while deliberately sidestepping the entertainment industry's celebrity spotlight.

Early Life, Background, and Age

Alex Cowper-Smith was born in October 1981 in London, England. Raised in an accomplished family with deep ties to British commerce and civic society, he enjoyed a privileged and academically rigorous upbringing. As of 2026, Alex Cowper-Smith is 45 years old.

During his adolescent years, Cowper-Smith attended the prestigious Westminster School, an ancient independent boarding school situated within the precincts of Westminster Abbey in central London. It was at Westminster that he first crossed paths with Alice Eve, daughter of famed British actors Trevor Eve and Sharon Maughan. The two teenagers formed an immediate bond, dating throughout their Sixth Form years before university and early career ambitions led them on divergent life paths.

Following his graduation from Westminster School, Cowper-Smith enrolled at the University of Nottingham, one of Britain's top research universities, where he studied Business, Economics, and Finance. He graduated with honors in the mid-2000s, equipped with the quantitative expertise required to enter London's fiercely competitive financial hub, the City of London.

Career and Professional Achievements at Goldman Sachs

Upon completing his academic training, Alex Cowper-Smith joined the ranks of Goldman Sachs, universally recognized as one of the world's preeminent multinational investment banking and securities firms. Working out of the firm's major European headquarters in London, he served as a financial associate and banking executive within corporate finance and capital management divisions.

In this high-stakes capacity, Cowper-Smith was tasked with evaluating complex cross-border mergers and acquisitions, structuring balance sheet debt, and advising corporate institutional clients on public capital raises. The demanding workload of Goldman Sachs banking associates—routinely exceeding 80 to 90 hours per week—requires immense endurance and mental resilience. Cowper-Smith earned high praise from institutional clients for his numerical precision, strategic foresight, and absolute confidentiality.

Marriage to Alice Eve: High School Romance, London Wedding, and Divorce

More than a decade after their initial high school romance at Westminster School, fate brought Alex Cowper-Smith and Alice Eve back together in early 2014. By that time, Eve had established herself as a celebrated international actress following her standout performance as Dr. Carol Marcus in J.J. Abrams' blockbuster Star Trek Into Darkness, while Cowper-Smith had solidified his reputation in London banking.

Their rekindled romance progressed swiftly. In August 2014, the couple announced their engagement, sparking widespread interest across British and American entertainment media. On New Year's Eve, December 31, 2014, Alex Cowper-Smith and Alice Eve were married in a picturesque, traditional winter ceremony at the historic Brompton Oratory in South Kensington, London. Surrounded by close family, childhood friends, and prominent British screen actors, the wedding was hailed as a romantic fairytale reunion of high school sweethearts.

However, the realities of balancing two extraordinarily demanding careers in different parts of the world soon placed severe strain on the marriage. While Cowper-Smith was tied to intensive desk-bound hours and financial transactions in London, Eve's burgeoning film career required months of continuous international location shooting in Los Angeles, New York, and Vancouver. Much like the cross-continental pressures explored in our biography of Jennifer Lynton and Marlene Knaus, the physical distance proved difficult to bridge.

In 2017, after approximately three years of marriage, Alex Cowper-Smith and Alice Eve quietly separated and finalized their divorce. The dissolution was handled with exemplary maturity and mutual respect, with both parties issuing no acrimonious statements and maintaining amicable terms in private.

Children and Family Life

Alex Cowper-Smith and Alice Eve did not have any children during their three-year marriage. Following their divorce, both individuals prioritized their respective professional trajectories while preserving the privacy of their families and mutual childhood networks in London.

Alex Cowper-Smith Net Worth and Financial Standing

Through his lucrative career in tier-one investment banking and corporate advisory, Alex Cowper-Smith has accrued an estimated personal net worth of $2 Million to $3 Million. High-level associates and financiers at institutions like Goldman Sachs earn substantial compensation packages consisting of competitive base salaries, performance-driven annual bonuses, and equity participation.

By comparison, his former spouse Alice Eve maintains an estimated net worth of approximately $6 Million, derived from major film salaries, television contracts (including Marvel's Iron Fist and Belgravia), and commercial endorsements. Their divorce settlement was concluded privately without public financial disputes, allowing both to move forward on solid economic footings.

Where Is Alex Cowper-Smith Today?

Today, Alex Cowper-Smith resides in London, where he continues to operate as a senior corporate financial consultant and private advisor to British enterprises. Following the media flurry surrounding his marriage and divorce from Alice Eve, he deliberately retreated entirely from the public eye, avoiding red-carpet events and maintaining an understated presence.

He does not maintain public social media accounts, focusing his energy entirely on corporate advisory, charitable community projects in London, and his lifelong personal friendships. His career remains a testament to high-level British financial expertise and personal dignity.
