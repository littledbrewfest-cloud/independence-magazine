Cody Herpin: Jodie Sweetin’s Ex-Husband, Behind-the-Scenes Film Career, and Fatherhood

An investigative editorial profile of Cody Herpin: his career as a film transportation coordinator and prop technician, marriage to Full House actress Jodie Sweetin, co-parenting daughter Zoie, and life today.

By Sarah Jenkins
Senior Lifestyle & Culture Writer
9 min read October 27, 2026

In the fast-moving universe of Hollywood celebrity marriages, media narratives frequently reduce complex human partnerships to fleeting tabloid headlines. Cody Herpin experienced the full intensity of that glare during the late 2000s when he married beloved television star Jodie Sweetin—famed globally for her childhood portrayal of Stephanie Tanner on the iconic ABC sitcom Full House. Yet before and after his high-profile marriage, Herpin was an established, hardworking film and television industry technician whose practical expertise in transportation logistics, prop coordination, and set operations supported dozens of independent and studio productions. His life story reflects the reality of a dedicated father who navigated unexpected public scrutiny while remaining committed to his work and daughter.

Much like other grounded professionals who experienced temporary media frenzies through celebrity marriages—such as Norman Kali navigating Hollywood production alongside Evangeline Lilly or Ashton Meem rebuilding personal privacy—Cody Herpin stepped away from public speculation to prioritize private stability and long-term parental responsibility.

Early Life, Background, and Technical Training

Cody Herpin was born on April 28, 1977, in California. Growing up in the greater Los Angeles area, he developed an early enthusiasm for mechanics, automobiles, and physical construction. Rather than pursuing performing arts or theoretical academia, Herpin possessed a hands-on disposition, learning practical automotive maintenance, heavy transport logistics, and technical carpentry throughout his late teens and early twenties.

His technical skills naturally opened doors to the Southern California entertainment production infrastructure. Film and television studios constantly require reliable, punctual crew members who understand the intricate logistics of moving delicate film equipment, transporting talent safely to remote shooting locations, and fabricating on-set props under tight schedules. Herpin earned a reputation among studio department heads as an industrious, calm technician who got jobs done efficiently without drama.

Hollywood Production Career: Transportation and Art Department Roles

Throughout the 2000s, Herpin built an impressive technical resume across major studio and independent motion picture sets. He served on the transportation and art departments of various notable film projects, including the post-apocalyptic action feature Resident Evil: Extinction (2007), starring Milla Jovovich, the thriller Dark Honeymoon (2008), family comedy Alice Upside Down (2007), and horror production Unearthed (2007).

His responsibilities often involved coordinating picture cars, operating specialized transport trailers, and assisting set decorators with props and mechanical rigging. While audience members focus solely on famous actors on screen, industry veterans recognize that crew members like Herpin are indispensable for maintaining the seamless daily operations of Hollywood film sets.

Meeting Jodie Sweetin on Set and Their 2007 Wedding

In May 2007, Cody Herpin crossed paths with actress Jodie Sweetin through mutual colleagues in the television production community. Sweetin, who had divorced her first husband Shaun Holguin in 2006, was actively working to rebuild her life and career following well-documented personal struggles with substance abuse during her early twenties.

Sweetin found comfort in Herpin’s down-to-earth demeanor, dry humor, and grounded lifestyle outside Hollywood’s party circuit. The romance developed rapidly; after just two months of dating, the couple traveled to Las Vegas, Nevada, exchanging vows in an intimate ceremony on July 14, 2007. Sweetin spoke glowingly to entertainment reporters about Herpin’s supportive presence, frequently stating that his steady nature gave her the emotional security she needed to begin a new chapter.

The Birth of Daughter Zoie Laurel May Herpin

On April 12, 2008, the couple welcomed their first child, a daughter named Zoie Laurel May Herpin, delivered via an emergency C-section in Los Angeles. The arrival of baby Zoie brought immense happiness to both parents, and Herpin was deeply devoted to hands-on fatherhood from her first moments.

In interviews during that period, Sweetin proudly described Herpin as an exceptional father who handled midnight feedings and diaper changes with patient dedication. Photographs from red carpet charity premieres showed Herpin standing proudly alongside Sweetin and their baby daughter, projecting a warm, united family front to the world.

High-Profile Divorce, Custody Agreements, and Co-Parenting

However, the rapid nature of their romance, financial pressures, and differing long-term expectations soon introduced severe strains into the marriage. In November 2008, just seven months after Zoie’s birth and sixteen months after their wedding, Sweetin filed for legal separation in Orange County Superior Court, which evolved into a contentious divorce battle covered extensively by entertainment tabloids.

Herpin contested custody arrangements, seeking primary custody to ensure Zoie’s safety and daily stability during a turbulent period in Sweetin’s personal life. The legal proceedings continued for nearly two years before being formally finalized on April 20, 2010. Ultimately, both parents agreed to a comprehensive joint custody arrangement. Over time, as media interest faded, both Herpin and Sweetin matured into a cooperative co-parenting dynamic, putting Zoie’s emotional and academic needs first.

Career After the Spotlight and Personal Growth

Following the finalization of his divorce, Cody Herpin deliberately retreated from public exposure. He avoided reality television offers, declined sensational tell-all interviews, and returned to his core vocation in technical production, mechanical services, and automotive logistics in Southern California.

In 2026, Herpin’s daughter Zoie is 18 years old, celebrating her high school graduation and stepping forward into young adulthood. Family acquaintances consistently note that Herpin has remained a constant, loving presence in Zoie’s life, attending her school activities, milestones, and celebrations quietly away from cameras.

Behind the Scenes: The Technical Craft of Film Transportation

In the entertainment industry, the role of a transportation technician and picture car specialist requires an intricate balance of logistical precision and safety coordination. Operating under Teamsters standards, drivers and equipment coordinators are responsible for moving millions of dollars in camera rigs, sound stages, lighting trailers, and specialized stunt vehicles across challenging shooting terrains.

Throughout his career on demanding sets like Resident Evil: Extinction—which filmed in rigorous desert conditions in Mexico and the American Southwest—Herpin demonstrated the endurance, technical problem-solving, and punctual reliability that high-budget productions rely on. Those who worked alongside him on set recall that while movie stars commanded the public spotlight, crew members like Herpin worked eighteen-hour shifts ensuring every mechanical asset was primed and ready for the director's call.

Life in 2026: Maturity, Independence, and Private Stability

Today in 2026, at 49 years old, Cody Herpin has built a peaceful, grounded existence in Southern California. Having weathered the intense media storm of a public celebrity divorce early in his thirties, he developed a profound appreciation for discretion and personal boundaries. Friends describe him as an avid outdoorsman and devoted mechanic who enjoys working on classic cars, spending quality time with family, and pursuing practical projects away from the digital noise of social media.

His story serves as an instructive example of personal resilience. Rather than allowing a brief celebrity marriage to define his lifetime trajectory or pursuing fleeting internet fame, Herpin embraced his authentic calling as a skilled technician and committed father, proving that long-term character is forged through quiet, daily responsibility.

Financial Profile and Estimated Net Worth

As of 2026, financial biographical analysts estimate Cody Herpin’s personal net worth at between $300,000 and $500,000 USD. This estimate is based on his multi-decade career as an independent film technician, commercial transportation contractor, and technical specialist in California.
