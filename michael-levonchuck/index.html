Who Is Michael Levonchuck? Inside Amber Rose’s Father’s Life, Military Service, and Family Roots

A complete biographical look at Michael Levonchuck: his South Philadelphia roots, Italian and Irish heritage, twenty years of military service, and his enduring relationship with daughter Amber Rose.

By Marcus Vance
Chief Investigative Journalist & Senior Biographer
10 min read November 3, 2026

Michael Levonchuck is an American military veteran of Italian and Irish descent, best known as the father of model, author, and media personality Amber Rose. Born and raised in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, he served twenty years in the United States military and built a private life rooted in South Philadelphia, maintaining a quiet, enduring bond with his daughter and grandson.

While Amber Rose gained worldwide celebrity across hip-hop culture, fashion runways, and television, her father's background is grounded in blue-collar discipline and military service. His Italian-American heritage provided an important cultural foundation for Amber during her formative years.

Similar to other family patriarchs who chose personal privacy over Hollywood glare—such as Michael Cannata Jr. honoring family roots through quiet dedication or Shelley Covel Rowland maintaining grounded values within a celebrated lineage—Michael Levonchuck exemplifies working-class integrity and fatherly devotion.

Who Is Michael Levonchuck?

Michael Levonchuck is an American military veteran who spent two decades serving in the armed forces. He is known to the public as the father of Amber Rose (born Amber Levonchuck), whose fame across fashion, music videos, and reality television brought attention to her multicultural Philadelphia family.

Unlike many celebrity parents who embrace public exposure, Michael has consistently shunned the spotlight. He has lived an unassuming life in Pennsylvania, valuing privacy, military camaraderie, and family ties.

Friends and relatives describe him as a disciplined, hardworking individual who instilled resilience and self-reliance in his children despite the economic challenges they faced in Philadelphia.

His Family’s Connection to Philadelphia

Michael's family history is tied closely to the working-class neighborhoods of South Philadelphia. The area is renowned for its vibrant immigrant heritage, strong community ties, and historic Italian-American enclaves.

Growing up in Philadelphia during the 1960s and 1970s, Michael learned the values of neighborhood loyalty and grit. Those qualities guided his decision to enlist in the military as a young man, seeking both service to his country and professional trade skills.

His deep Philadelphia roots later influenced Amber Rose, who frequently speaks with pride about her hometown's influence on her fierce independence and street smarts.

Amber Rose addressing a public audience, highlighting the resilience and work ethic passed down from her father Michael Levonchuck.

Michael Levonchuck’s Irish and Italian Roots

Michael's cultural lineage is an amalgamation of Italian and Irish ancestry. His parents and grandparents were part of the wave of European immigrants who settled in Pennsylvania's industrial corridors during the twentieth century.

The Levonchuck household celebrated traditional Sunday family dinners, Catholic heritage, and Italian culinary traditions. This rich heritage formed half of Amber Rose's distinctive multi-ethnic background.

On her mother Dorothy Rose's side, Amber inherits Cape Verdean and Scottish ancestry, creating a unique cultural blend that Amber has celebrated openly in discussions about modern American diversity.

His Marriage to Dorothy Rose and Military Service

Michael met Dorothy Rose in Philadelphia during the late 1970s. The couple married and welcomed their daughter, Amber Levonchuck, on October 21, 1983. They also raised a son, Antonio Hewlett.

Throughout their early marriage, Michael served on active duty in the military, which frequently required relocations and deployments. His twenty years of service earned him respect among peers, providing structured discipline and veterans' benefits for his household.

However, the strains of military life and economic hardships eventually led to Michael and Dorothy's divorce when Amber was an adolescent. Following the divorce, Dorothy raised the children as a single mother in South Philadelphia.

Military Discipline and Values Passed Down to Amber

Despite the challenges of separation, Michael's military background left an indelible mark on his children. He emphasized punctuality, physical resilience, and emotional stoicism, traits that Amber relied upon as she entered the cutthroat entertainment industry.

Amber has recalled in interviews how her father taught her to never fear hard work and to maintain dignity under adversity. When Amber navigated early career controversies, Michael provided private counsel focused on perseverance and family pride.

The lessons in personal accountability from her father helped Amber establish her successful publishing ventures, business sponsorships, and non-profit advocacy organizations.

His Relationship with Daughter Amber Rose and Grandchildren

In adulthood, Michael and Amber maintained a supportive, loving bond. When Amber became a mother, Michael embraced his role as grandfather to Sebastian Taylor Thomaz, born in 2013, and Slash Electric Edwards, born in 2019.

Amber has occasionally shared family photographs showing Michael teaching his grandsons Philadelphia traditions and sharing quiet holiday afternoons away from media scrutiny.

These private family reunions demonstrate how enduring bloodlines remain despite the vast differences between quiet military retirement and high-voltage celebrity culture.

Preserving Italian-American Heritage Across Generations

Growing up in a tight-knit Italian enclave in South Philadelphia, Michael developed a lifelong appreciation for traditional recipes, community festivals, and multi-generational family gatherings. He learned the value of loyalty and neighborhood camaraderie, values he worked to pass along to his daughter Amber.

Even as Amber gained international fame and established a glamorous career in Hollywood and New York, Michael ensured that his grandchildren remained connected to their East Coast heritage. Family dinners featuring homemade Italian pasta and traditional holiday customs remain a cherished part of their private reunions.

Life Today and Military Retirement

Following his retirement after twenty years of honorable military service, Michael Levonchuck chose to remain in the Pennsylvania region. He enjoys a peaceful retirement supported by military pension benefits and private investments.

He spends his time engaged in veterans' community groups, family gatherings, and following Philadelphia sports teams. He remains steadfast in his choice to stay away from reality television cameras and celebrity interviews.

His quiet lifestyle provides an important anchor for Amber Rose, who views her father's home as a sanctuary from the relentless pressures of media scrutiny.

Paternal Support and Lifelong Family Bond

Through every phase of Amber's public career—from high-profile relationships to television hosting and social activism—Michael has remained a steady, non-judgmental presence in her life. Amber has repeatedly emphasized that her father's unwavering acceptance helped her weather public criticism with composure.

Rather than seeking financial favors or commercial exploitation of his daughter's brand, Michael takes pride simply in seeing his daughter thrive as an independent businesswoman and devoted mother to his two grandsons.

Summary and Lasting Perspective

Michael Levonchuck represents the steadfast dedication of millions of American military service members who quietly perform their duties away from public applause. His service to the nation and his Italian-American heritage shaped the character of his family.

While his daughter Amber Rose navigates the upper tiers of global entertainment, Michael's steady presence in South Philadelphia remains a powerful reminder of her working-class roots. His story is one of service, humility, and enduring paternal love.
