Who Is Ian Alexander Sr.? The Biography of Regina King's Ex-Husband, Career, and Life Today

Decades of executive leadership in television distribution, his marriage to Regina King, and the enduring legacy of their son.

By Marcus Vance
Investigative Features & Senior Editor
8 min read October 11, 2026

Behind the glare of red carpets, Academy Awards, and prime-time Emmy triumphs, Regina King has consistently pointed to her family as the true foundation of her life. During the pivotal decade when King transitioned from acclaimed child star on NBC's 227 to one of Hollywood's most respected film actors and directors, her partner in both marriage and parenthood was television distribution executive Ian Alexander Sr.

Unlike many spouses who gravitate toward the celebrity spotlight, Alexander built a substantial corporate career behind the cameras. As an executive at Warner Bros. Television and international distribution networks, he helped manage syndicated programming while co-parenting their beloved son, Ian Alexander Jr. His life reflects professional substance, dignity, and a profound commitment to family.

Early Life and Career in Television Syndication

Ian Alexander Sr. was born in 1956, spending his formative years between Canada and Southern California. Blessed with a natural charisma and keen communication skills, he initially explored creative arts and acting in the early 1980s, making appearances in television productions such as the acclaimed police procedural Hill Street Blues.

However, Alexander quickly realized that long-term power in Hollywood resided in business administration and broadcast syndication. He transitioned to the corporate sector, rising through the ranks of television distribution. He served as Vice President of Distribution for Warner Bros. Television and later held senior distribution roles with QVC and international cable networks, licensing television libraries across domestic and foreign markets.

Pioneering Leadership in African American Television Syndication

During the 1990s and early 2000s, executive suites in major Hollywood television studios remained largely homogenous. Ian Alexander Sr. was among a pioneering group of African American corporate executives who navigated the high-stakes world of national broadcast syndication and licensing at Warner Bros. Television. In this role, Alexander was responsible for packaging and negotiating lucrative distribution rights for scripted network series, classic television libraries, and first-run syndicated programming across regional affiliate stations nationwide.

His business acumen required deep mathematical modeling of advertising ratings, demographic viewer shares, and market carriage fees. Colleagues respected his quiet authority, sharp negotiating instincts, and encyclopedic knowledge of broadcast programming cycles. He later transitioned his expertise into modern cable retail and international distribution, advising corporate networks on global content monetization.

Relationship and 1997 Marriage to Regina King

Alexander began dating Regina King in the late 1980s, when King was coming off her successful five-season run on 227 and establishing herself as a formidable dramatic actress with breakout roles in John Singleton's Boyz n the Hood (1991), Poetic Justice (1993), and Cameron Crowe's Jerry Maguire (1996).

On January 19, 1996, the couple welcomed their only child together, a son named Ian Alexander Jr. A little over a year later, on April 23, 1997, the couple married in an intimate destination ceremony in Jamaica, surrounded by their closest family members and friends.

The 2007 Divorce: From Bitterness to Unconditional Co-Parenting

In 2007, after a decade of marriage, Regina King and Ian Alexander Sr. finalized their divorce. In her 2017 contribution to the book He Never Came Home, King wrote with remarkable candor about the initial friction following their separation.

However, King recounted a turning point when both parents attended one of their son's Little League baseball games. Their commitment to mutual respect and active co-parenting bears strong resemblance to the relationship between Wanda Hutchins and Michael Strahan, who maintained a unified parenting team across three decades.

Seeing their tension affect Ian Jr., Alexander walked over to King, suggesting they cast aside their differences and present a united front of love and stability for their son. From that day forward, the two developed an extraordinarily close friendship, frequently attending family dinners, school events, and holiday gatherings as a unified parenting team.

Ian Alexander Jr.'s Musical Journey as DJ 'Desduné'

Ian Alexander Jr. inherited his father's sharp ear for audio production and his mother's creative magnetism. Rather than pursuing traditional Hollywood acting, Ian Jr. immersed himself in electronic music production, synthesizer composition, and DJing, performing under the stage name 'Desduné.' He developed a dedicated following across Los Angeles and New York clubs, blending deep house, afrobeat, and soul into sophisticated soundscapes.

Ian Sr. took immense pride in his son's independent artistic development, frequently discussing production techniques and sound engineering with him. When Ian Jr. accompanied Regina King to the 91st Academy Awards in 2019, his affectionate red-carpet interviews praising both of his parents went viral, capturing the hearts of millions and reflecting the loving, supportive environment his parents had built despite their divorce.

The Legacy of Ian Alexander Jr. (1996–2022)

Their son, Ian Alexander Jr., blossomed into an extraordinarily talented artist, musician, and DJ, performing under the stage name "Desduné." With an engaging personality and deep musical intuition, Ian Jr. regularly escorted his mother to the Golden Globes and Academy Awards, celebrating her Oscar win for If Beale Street Could Talk in 2019.

Tragically, in January 2022, just two days after his twenty-sixth birthday, Ian Alexander Jr. died by suicide. In the wake of this devastating loss, Regina King and Ian Alexander Sr. stood together in profound, united grief. In public statements and tributes, both parents emphasized their immense pride in their son's bright spirit, kindness, and artistic genius.

Global Television Markets and MIPCOM Cannes Licensing

Throughout his executive career at Warner Bros. Television, Ian Alexander Sr. was a regular presence at premier international television trade markets, most notably MIPCOM and MIPTV held annually in Cannes, France. In these high-pressure deal-making forums, Alexander negotiated cross-border syndication rights with European, Asian, and Latin American broadcast networks.

His ability to bridge cultural differences and package diverse television catalogs into commercially viable international broadcast bundles established him as one of the most trusted distribution minds in television. Industry veterans praised his foresight in recognizing the early shift toward global digital rights and specialized cable network licensing.

Professional Life Today and Estimated Net Worth

Today, Ian Alexander Sr. lives a quiet, private life in California. His preference for corporate discretion away from red-carpet vanity mirrors the life philosophies of Diane Addonizio and Marlene Knaus, who prioritized family foundations above public recognition.

He continues to provide consulting services for independent media distribution companies and television content licensing networks, avoiding the public eye while honoring the memory of his son.

His personal net worth is estimated between $3 Million and $4 Million, accrued through decades of executive-level compensation in television syndication, licensing commissions, and long-term Southern California real estate investments.
