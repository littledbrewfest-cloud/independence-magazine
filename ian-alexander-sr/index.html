Who Is Ian Alexander Sr.? The Life, Music Career, and Journey of Regina King’s Ex-Husband

Beyond the tabloid headlines: A deep retrospective on the Warner Bros VP, his ten-year marriage to Oscar-winner Regina King, and the enduring bond they shared.

By David H. Miller
Chief Cultural & Society Biographer
7 min read October 10, 2026

When Regina King took the Dolby Theatre stage in 2019 to accept her Academy Award for If Beale Street Could Talk, her eyes swept across the auditorium with the grounded wisdom of a seasoned artist. Walking closely beside her through every chapter of her formative adulthood was music industry executive Ian Alexander Sr.

Though mainstream audiences primarily know his name through his high-profile ten-year marriage to King, Alexander built a substantial, highly respected career behind the scenes of major record labels, pioneering distribution frameworks for Quincy Jones' Qwest Records and Warner Bros Records.

Early Life and Canadian Roots

Ian Alexander Sr. was born on April 8, 1956, in the mountainous terrain of Kamloops, British Columbia, Canada. Developing a keen interest in both audio production and corporate administration, Alexander moved from British Columbia into media syndication, working in Canadian broadcasting before relocating to Los Angeles in the late 1980s.

Executive Career at Warner Bros and Qwest Records

In Los Angeles, Alexander’s strategic mind and deep appreciation for R&B, jazz, and hip-hop quickly caught the attention of major label heads. He rose through corporate ranks to become Vice President of Distribution at Qwest Records, the legendary boutique imprint founded by Quincy Jones in partnership with Warner Music Group.

In this executive seat, Alexander oversaw retail distribution, domestic marketing campaigns, and contract compliance for multiple charting projects. His colleagues regarded him as an astute negotiator who prioritized artist royalties and sustained commercial longevity over fleeting trends.

Meeting Regina King and Their 1997 Marriage

Alexander and Regina King first met in 1994, during a period when King was transitioning from celebrated child star (227) into an authoritative Hollywood powerhouse with roles in Boyz n the Hood, Poetic Justice, and Jerry Maguire.

The couple welcomed their only child, Ian Alexander Jr., on January 19, 1996. One year later, on April 23, 1997, they married in a sophisticated Jamaican ceremony attended by close family and Hollywood luminaries. Throughout the late 1990s and early 2000s, Alexander was King’s constant companion on premier red carpets, supporting her transitions into television directing and film producing.

The 2007 Separation and Evolutionary Co-Parenting

In 2006, after nearly a decade of marriage, the couple separated, finalizing their divorce in 2007. While early post-split tensions were documented in the media, both Alexander and King made a deliberate, conscious choice to prioritize their son above personal grievances.

In her 2017 essay for Essence, King publicly praised Alexander, revealing that during their son's Little League baseball games, they had a breakthrough realization. "We were both sitting on opposite sides of the bleachers," King recalled. "We looked at each other and realized our anger was punishing Ian. Right then, we agreed to be partners in our son's happiness." Over the subsequent fifteen years, Alexander and King became close friends, celebrating holidays and milestones as an integrated family unit.

The Heartbreaking Loss of Ian Alexander Jr. (2022)

In January 2022, shortly after his 26th birthday, their son Ian Alexander Jr., an accomplished musician, DJ, and chef, tragically passed away. The profound tragedy brought global condolences from President Barack Obama, Ava DuVernay, and millions of fans.

In the quiet aftermath, Alexander Sr. and King supported one another with immense grace. King described her late son as "a bright light who cared so deeply about the happiness of others," an enduring sentiment shared deeply by Alexander Sr.

Net Worth & Current Endeavors

Ian Alexander Sr.'s net worth is estimated at $3 Million to $4 Million, built on his executive severance and profit-sharing at Warner Bros, private music consulting, and real estate investments in California. Today, he maintains a quiet, peaceful life outside of Los Angeles, actively supporting youth music non-profits and mentorship programs.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What was Ian Alexander Sr.'s role in the music industry?

He served as Vice President of Distribution at Qwest Records and held senior distribution roles at Warner Bros Records.

How long were Regina King and Ian Alexander Sr. married?

They were married for 10 years, from April 1997 until their divorce was finalized in 2007.

Did Ian Alexander Sr. act in movies?

He had brief cameo appearances in projects like the 2005 series Masters of Horror, but his primary career was as a corporate music distribution executive.
