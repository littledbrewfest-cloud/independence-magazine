Ian Alexander Sr. Biography: Career, 10-Year Marriage to Regina King, and Life Today

From senior distribution executive roles at Warner Bros and Qwest Records to his decade-long marriage with Academy Award winner Regina King.

While Regina King has long been celebrated as one of Hollywood's most acclaimed actresses and directors, her former husband, Ian Alexander Sr., played a notable behind-the-scenes role in the American music industry during the 1990s and early 2000s.

Alexander built a respected executive career in record distribution, managing partnerships with major labels and contributing to prominent artist releases before choosing a quieter path outside corporate entertainment.

Early Life and Career in the Music Industry

Ian Alexander Sr. was born on April 8, 1956, in Kamloops, British Columbia, Canada. Demonstrating a natural aptitude for marketing and logistics, he moved to California in the late 1980s to work in the recording industry.

He served as Vice President of Distribution at Qwest Records, the influential label founded by legendary producer Quincy Jones. During his tenure, Alexander oversaw album supply chains, retail partnerships, and international distribution before transitioning into corporate distribution projects with Warner Bros Records.

Marriage to Regina King (1997–2007)

Alexander met Regina King in the mid-1990s as her acting career gained momentum with major roles in Boyz n the Hood, Friday, and Jerry Maguire. On April 23, 1997, they married in a private ceremony in Jamaica.

Throughout their ten-year marriage, they supported each other's careers while raising their son, Ian Alexander Jr., born in January 1996. Although the couple divorced in 2007, they maintained a dedicated, cooperative relationship as co-parents.

Life Today and Net Worth

Today, Alexander maintains a low public profile, residing in Southern California where he provides private music business consulting and supports community music initiatives. His estimated net worth stands between $3 Million and $4 Million.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What was Ian Alexander Sr.'s executive position in music?

He served as Vice President of Distribution at Quincy Jones's Qwest Records and held distribution roles at Warner Bros Records.

How long were Regina King and Ian Alexander Sr. married?

They were married for ten years, from 1997 until their divorce in 2007.
