Who Is Mia Weier? The Biography of Surfing Star Kanoa Igarashi's Former Partner and Life Today

From coastal California roots to supporting Kanoa Igarashi on the WSL Championship Tour and Olympic Silver in Tokyo.

Cultural Biographer & Senior Arts Critic

In the global arena of professional surfing, Japanese-American phenom Kanoa Igarashi stands as an international icon—an elite World Surf League (WSL) Championship Tour contender and Olympic Silver Medalist at the Tokyo 2020 Games. During the critical four-year window when Igarashi cemented his status among the world's premier wave-riders, Mia Weier was a central figure in his personal journey, accompanying him across the international surf circuit from Tahiti to Hawaii.

Known for her understated elegance, natural coastal California aesthetic, and commitment to environmental conservation, Weier earned admiration from surfing communities across North America and Japan. Following their quiet parting in 2023, she has continued to build an independent profile centered on sustainable living, fashion curation, and ocean conservation.

Early Life and Coastal California Roots

Mia Weier was born in 1999 in Santa Barbara, California, a coastal community renowned for its historic surf breaks and environmentally conscious culture. Growing up along the Pacific coastline, she developed a passion for ocean ecology, swimming, and wellness from an early age.

Weier pursued studies in communications and environmental studies, developing a keen eye for visual aesthetics, photography, and sustainable apparel. Friends from Southern California describe her as grounded, artistic, and deeply committed to natural lifestyle practices.

Meeting Kanoa Igarashi and the International Surf Tour

In 2019, Mia crossed paths with Kanoa Igarashi through mutual friends in the Southern California surf scene. Born in Huntington Beach to Japanese surfing parents, Igarashi was already competing at the highest tiers of the WSL Championship Tour.

Their connection quickly developed into a prominent partnership. Mia accompanied Igarashi on grueling world tour stops, including the Billabong Pro Teahupo'o in Tahiti, the Vans Triple Crown of Surfing on Oahu's North Shore, the MEO Rip Curl Pro in Portugal, and the Rip Curl Pro Bells Beach in Australia. Her calming influence provided Igarashi with emotional equilibrium amid the intense pressures of competitive world rankings.

The High-Performance Demands of the WSL Championship Tour

Professional surfing at the World Surf League (WSL) Championship Tour level is one of the most physically and logistically demanding disciplines in global sports. Surfers do not compete on standard schedules; instead, competitions rely on multi-week waiting periods that track unpredictable ocean swell forecasts, wind directions, and tidal patterns across remote global locations. During their four-year partnership, Mia Weier was an integral part of Kanoa Igarashi's traveling support entourage.

From the heavy, dangerous reef barrels of Teahupo'o in French Polynesia to the freezing waters of Peniche, Portugal, Weier helped manage the logistical challenges of international travel, nutritional regimens, and board testing alongside famed surfboard shaper Channel Islands Surfboards in Santa Barbara. Her presence provided Igarashi with psychological balance and domestic normalcy in high-pressure competition environments where a single wave score can determine a year-long world title campaign.

The Tokyo 2020 Olympic Silver Medal Journey

The defining athletic milestone of Igarashi's career occurred during the Tokyo 2020 Olympic Games (held in the summer of 2021), where surfing made its historic debut as an official Olympic sport at Tsurigasaki Beach in Chiba, Japan. Representing Japan, Igarashi faced immense national expectations.

In an unforgettable semifinal heat, Igarashi executed a daring aerial maneuver to defeat world champion Gabriel Medina, securing the Olympic Silver Medal for host nation Japan. Throughout the intense training camps and strict COVID-19 bio-bubble protocols leading up to the Games, Mia served as his principal domestic supporter, celebrating his historic achievement alongside his family.

Quiet 2023 Separation and Mature Independence

In mid-2023, after four years together, Mia Weier and Kanoa Igarashi quietly ended their relationship. Unlike many young influencer couples, neither party engaged in public drama, cryptic social media attacks, or tabloid discussions.

Both removed shared couple photographs from their respective platforms with mutual respect, choosing to focus on their respective professional paths without animosity.

Commitment to Marine Conservation and Coastal Ecosystems

Growing up on the beaches of Santa Barbara, Mia Weier developed a lifelong dedication to marine biology and ocean stewardship that extends far beyond surfing culture. She has actively volunteered with California marine non-profit organizations focused on giant kelp forest restoration, coastal wetland preservation, and marine debris cleanup along the Channel Islands coastline.

In her professional creative work, Weier collaborates exclusively with sustainable lifestyle brands that champion biodegradable packaging, ocean-recovered plastic textiles, and reef-safe sunscreen formulations. Her authentic connection to ocean ecology has made her a respected young voice among eco-conscious California creatives seeking to protect fragile marine habitats for future generations.

Visual Storytelling and Coastal Photography Portfolio

In addition to her environmental advocacy, Mia Weier has cultivated an evocative visual portfolio as a coastal lifestyle photographer and creative director. Drawing inspiration from 1970s analog surf photography and the pristine natural landscapes of California's Central Coast, her work captures unposed, intimate moments of ocean culture, sea life, and coastal flora.

Her editorial features and digital photo essays have been showcased by independent environmental publications and ethical surf collectives, celebrating marine minimalism and mindful living. Collectors and collaborators commend her authentic aesthetic, which avoids commercial superficiality in favor of deep environmental reverence.

Creative Pursuits, Ocean Stewardship, and Life Today

Brand Collaborations and Environmental Advocacy

In her ongoing creative work, Mia Weier partners with ethical outdoor brands, organic skincare formulations, and sustainable surf labels that share her environmental philosophy. Refusing commercial sponsorships that rely on synthetic microplastics or petroleum-derived packaging, she has used her digital influence to promote consumer mindfulness, zero-waste beach culture, and marine habitat awareness across California.

Her collaborative projects emphasize long-term ecological balance over fast-fashion consumption, resonating with a growing demographic of conscious coastal residents who seek authenticity and nature-centric design.

Today, Mia Weier resides in Southern California, focusing on creative consulting, sustainable fashion curation, and ocean conservation projects. She works with eco-conscious lifestyle brands that prioritize ocean-safe materials, ethical manufacturing, and clean beauty.

Her personal net worth is estimated between $500,000 and $800,000, earned through creative brand partnerships, digital marketing consulting, and lifestyle curation projects.
