Who Is LaVelda Fann? Inside Robert Conrad’s Former Wife’s Life, Pageant Days, and Family

A complete biographical profile of LaVelda Fann: her Kentucky pageant beginnings, twenty-seven-year marriage to actor Robert Conrad, raising three daughters, and life today in Bear Valley.

By Marcus Vance
Chief Investigative Journalist & Senior Biographer
10 min read November 3, 2026

LaVelda Fann is an American former actress, beauty queen, and television personality best known as the second wife of television icon Robert Conrad. Married to Conrad from 1983 to 2010, she raised their three daughters—Kaja, Camille, and Chelsea—in Bear Valley, California, while pursuing interior design and community initiatives.

During the height of her husband's legendary career on classic television hits like The Wild Wild West and Baa Baa Black Sheep, LaVelda maintained an active role alongside him on set and in public life. She appeared on screen in several of Conrad's projects before transitioning to private business ventures.

Similar to other figures who navigated high-profile Hollywood marriages with resilience and personal autonomy—such as Steven Cauble prioritizing faith and family outside celebrity glare or Anna Kate Denver embracing Colorado community living—LaVelda Fann built a legacy defined by family devotion and quiet personal strength.

Who Is LaVelda Fann?

LaVelda Fann is an American former model and actress who rose to national prominence following her marriage to actor Robert Conrad. Over twenty-seven years of marriage, she was a recognized figure in Hollywood circles and within the High Sierra mountain community of California.

Raised with traditional Southern roots, LaVelda demonstrated poise and charisma from her youth. Her poise helped her win competitive regional pageants, opening pathways into modeling and entertainment opportunities.

Despite being decades younger than Conrad, LaVelda established a deeply collaborative partnership with him. Together, they navigated decades of television production, business ventures, and family life before amicably separating in 2010.

Her Early Life and Pageant Years

LaVelda was born in August 1960 in Alabama and raised across the American South. From early childhood, her charm, athletic grace, and speaking talent set her apart in local competitions.

During the 1970s, she competed in state and national youth pageants, eventually capturing the prestigious Miss National Teenager title. The victory provided national television exposure, college scholarships, and invitations to host charity events across the United States.

Her pageant achievements caught the attention of commercial casting directors, leading to commercial modeling assignments and introductory television guest appearances.

Robert Conrad and LaVelda Fann attending a Beverly Hills charity gala during their twenty-seven-year marriage.

How LaVelda Met Robert Conrad

LaVelda met Robert Conrad in the late 1970s when Conrad was emceeing a national beauty pageant in which she participated. Conrad, already a household name from Hawaiian Eye and The Wild Wild West, was struck by LaVelda's intelligence and warmth.

Their initial professional acquaintance developed into a deep romantic connection following Conrad's divorce from his first wife of twenty-five years, Joan Kenlay. In 1983, Robert and LaVelda married in an intimate ceremony attended by close family and friends.

Although entertainment media frequently commented on their twenty-five-year age gap, the couple proved their enduring commitment over nearly three decades of shared life.

Marriage and Their Three Daughters: Kaja, Camille, and Chelsea

During their marriage, Robert and LaVelda welcomed three daughters: Kaja, Camille, and Chelsea. LaVelda prioritized creating a stable, grounded environment for their children away from the frenetic pace of metropolitan Los Angeles.

All three daughters grew up inheriting their parents' artistic and athletic interests. Kaja Conrad pursued singing and songwriting, Camille explored creative arts, and Chelsea developed business and lifestyle endeavors.

In addition to raising her own three daughters, LaVelda maintained positive relations with Conrad's five adult children from his previous marriage, helping foster a united extended family.

Family Life in Bear Valley and the High Sierra

In the late 1980s, seeking peace and outdoor adventure, Robert and LaVelda relocated their primary family residence to Bear Valley, California. The alpine mountain setting in Alpine County provided an idyllic backdrop for skiing, horseback riding, and community involvement.

LaVelda became an active civic organizer in Bear Valley, coordinating local fundraisers and supporting volunteer emergency services. She also established her own interior decorating practice, designing rustic mountain homes and ski lodges.

The mountain lifestyle shaped their family identity, offering their daughters a wholesome childhood rooted in natural beauty rather than Hollywood red carpets.

LaVelda Fann’s Acting Career: High Sierra Search and Rescue

While primarily focused on family and local design work, LaVelda occasionally appeared on screen alongside her husband. In 1994, she co-starred in the television movie Search and Rescue and the subsequent 1995 NBC adventure drama series High Sierra Search and Rescue.

The series, filmed on location around Bear Valley, featured Conrad as mountain rescue leader Toops Sutter, with LaVelda playing fellow rescue team member Jessie Sutter. The project was a true family production, featuring appearances by several of their children.

Her performances received praise for their natural authenticity, demonstrating her ability to handle demanding physical scenes in rugged outdoor terrain.

Navigating Robert Conrad’s 2003 Accident and Long-Term Recovery

In March 2003, their family faced a serious test when Robert Conrad was involved in a near-fatal head-on car accident on Highway 4 in Calaveras County. The accident left Conrad with severe nerve damage and partial paralysis on the right side of his body.

Throughout his extensive surgeries and years of grueling physical therapy, LaVelda stepped into the role of primary caregiver and household anchor. She coordinated medical appointments, managed home health aides, and supported Conrad as he adapted to limited physical mobility.

Those close to the family noted that LaVelda's calm resolve and constant presence were instrumental in helping Conrad regain speech and limited motor function during a deeply challenging chapter of their lives.

Divorce in 2010 and Life in Recent Years

In 2010, after twenty-seven years of marriage, Robert Conrad and LaVelda Fann filed for divorce. Despite the legal dissolution, the separation remained respectful and cordial, with both parties continuing to support their daughters and grandchildren.

When Robert Conrad passed away from heart failure in Malibu on February 8, 2020, at the age of 84, LaVelda joined his children in honoring his cinematic legacy and memorable television career.

Today, LaVelda lives quietly in California, dedicating her time to her daughters, grandchildren, interior design consulting, and outdoor mountain preservation.

Summary and Lasting Perspective

LaVelda Fann's life is a testament to grace, versatility, and enduring maternal dedication. From Southern beauty pageants to High Sierra television productions and decades of family leadership, she carved out a distinctive identity.

Her contribution to Robert Conrad's life and her enduring devotion to her three daughters remain her defining accomplishments. In both Hollywood retrospectives and her local community, LaVelda is remembered with warmth and genuine respect.
