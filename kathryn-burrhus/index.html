The Secret History of Kathryn Burrhus: Meet Stone Cold Steve Austin’s First Wife and High School Sweetheart

Decades before 'Austin 3:16' defined wrestling history, Kathryn Burrhus was the quiet anchor of Steve Williams' life in Edna, Texas. Here is her untold story.

By Elena Vance
Entertainment & Pop Culture Historian
6 min read October 10, 2026

Before the black leather vests, the shattered glass entrance theme, and the multi-million dollar pay-per-view buyrates that transformed the World Wrestling Federation in the late 1990s, Steve Austin was simply Steve Williams—a bruising high school football player from rural Edna, Texas. And the love of his young life was Kathryn Burrhus.

While Austin’s subsequent marriages to Jeanie Clarke (Lady Blossom) and Debra McMichael generated relentless tabloid scrutiny across sports entertainment television, Kathryn Burrhus has remained a phantom figure: an authentic high-school sweetheart who witnessed the birth of a wrestling titan before quietly stepping away from the chaos.

The Edna High School Sweethearts

Growing up in Jackson County, Texas, Kathryn Burrhus was admired for her academic discipline, warm personality, and deep local roots. In the early 1980s at Edna High School, she met Steve Williams, who excelled as a standout running back for the Edna Cowboys.

Their courtship survived the transition to collegiate athletics. When Williams earned a football scholarship to Wharton County Junior College and subsequently transferred to the University of North Texas, Burrhus remained his primary confidante. In interviews years later, Austin fondly recounted that Burrhus was the person who encouraged his drive when knee injuries curtailed his dreams of entering the NFL.

The Grueling Dallas Sportatorium Days & 1990 Marriage

In 1989, Williams enrolled in Chris Adams’ wrestling school at the Dallas Sportatorium. Living on raw potatoes and sleeping in a beat-up car between independent bookings in the USWA, Williams relied heavily on Burrhus’s emotional and logistical backing.

On November 24, 1990, after nearly a decade of dating, the pair married in a quiet Texas ceremony. At the time, Williams was wrestling under the moniker "Stunning Steve Austin" in World Class Championship Wrestling, earning less than $20 a night while dreaming of national syndication.

The 1992 Separation and Annulment

The intense travel demands of professional wrestling quickly proved catastrophic for the young marriage. By 1991, Austin was signed to World Championship Wrestling (WCW) in Atlanta, spending more than 300 days a year on the road.

While touring the territories, Austin met English wrestling valet Jeanie Clarke (known on-screen as Lady Blossom). The emotional strain and cross-country distance proved insurmountable for Burrhus and Austin, leading to an annulment and legal divorce on August 7, 1992.

Kathryn Burrhus’s Life Today

Unlike many figures in the sports entertainment orbit, Burrhus never wrote an exposé, never accepted interview fees, and never capitalized on her ex-husband's ascension to becoming the biggest box-office draw in pro wrestling history.

She built a successful, respectable career in educational administration and corporate finance in Texas. Friends describe her as a woman of tremendous grace who looks back on her youthful years with Steve Williams with nostalgic warmth rather than bitterness.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Who was Steve Austin’s very first wife?

Kathryn Burrhus was Steve Austin’s first wife. They met in high school in Edna, Texas, and were married on November 24, 1990.

Did Steve Austin and Kathryn Burrhus have children together?

No. The couple did not have any children during their marriage.

Why did Kathryn Burrhus and Steve Austin divorce?

The 300-day annual travel schedule of professional wrestling and Austin's relationship with wrestling valet Jeanie Clarke led to their 1992 annulment.
