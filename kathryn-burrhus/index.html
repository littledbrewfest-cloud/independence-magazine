Who Is Kathryn Burrhus? Meet Stone Cold Steve Austin's First Wife and High School Sweetheart

Before the WWE stardom of Steve Austin, Kathryn Burrhus was his high school sweetheart and first wife in Edna, Texas.

By Elena Vance
Entertainment & Pop Culture Historian
6 min read October 10, 2026

Before Steve Austin became one of the biggest attractions in professional wrestling history, he was Steve Williams—a high school football athlete from Edna, Texas. In those early years, Kathryn Burrhus was his high school sweetheart and first wife.

Edna High School Roots

Kathryn Burrhus grew up in Jackson County, Texas, where she attended Edna High School. During their teenage years, she and Williams began dating as he played running back for the school football team.

When Williams enrolled at the University of North Texas, Burrhus remained an important figure in his life, encouraging his athletic pursuits even after knee injuries shifted his goals toward wrestling.

Marriage in 1990

On November 24, 1990, the couple married in Texas as Austin began wrestling under the name 'Stunning Steve Austin' in regional promotions. However, the demanding travel requirements of regional wrestling led to personal differences, and the marriage was annulled in August 1992.

Life After Marriage

Following their separation, Burrhus deliberately chose a private life away from sports entertainment. She built a career in administration in Texas and has maintained her privacy over the decades.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

When did Steve Austin and Kathryn Burrhus marry?

They married on November 24, 1990, in Texas.

Did they have children together?

No, they did not have children during their marriage.
