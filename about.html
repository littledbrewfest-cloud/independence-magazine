About the Publication

About Independence Magazine

Independence Magazine is a dedicated digital publication committed to substantive news reporting, comprehensive biographies of public figures, and investigative inquiry into technology, capital, and global culture.

Our Mission & Editorial Purpose

In an era saturated by automated click-aggregators and transient outrage, Independence Magazine was established on a single principle: truth and human depth cannot be automated.

Our newsroom specializes in long-form biography journalism—examining the real lives, critical turning points, private partnerships, and high-stakes decisions of the executives, artists, researchers, and public figures shaping our era. From Silicon Valley innovators to British cultural icons, we report with unvarnished accuracy, double-sourced facts, and narrative dignity.

Editorial Independence & Ownership

Independence Magazine maintains complete, uncompromised editorial separation between our newsroom and any commercial or partner interests.

  • No Pay-to-Play: We never accept payment, equity, or commercial gifts in exchange for favorable coverage or biographical inclusion.
  • Strict Separation: All commercial partnerships, advertisements, or programmatic networks are strictly segregated from editorial decision-making.
  • Double-Sourced Reporting: Every factual claim, biographical history, and financial estimate is verified through direct on-the-record sources or authoritative public records.

Fact-Checking & Verification Protocol

Every article published on Independence Magazine undergoes rigorous editorial review. We check claims against verified primary sources, state registries, corporate filings, and peer-reviewed studies. When sources request anonymity due to personal security or contractual nondisclosure agreements, their identities are confirmed directly by the Editor-in-Chief.
