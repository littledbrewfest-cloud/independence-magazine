Amanda Moye Brown: Age, Net Worth, Disney Career, Marriage to Wes Brown, and Life Today

Discover Amanda Moye Brown: her age, Disney-ABC entertainment career, marriage to Hallmark star Wes Brown, daughter Merribeth, and net worth.

By Marcus Vance
Culture & Society Features
8 min read October 15, 2026

Amanda Moye Brown is an accomplished American entertainment coordinator, production assistant, and music executive assistant who is widely recognized as the longtime wife of celebrated television and film actor Wes Brown. While Wes has become a beloved household name across millions of living rooms as a quintessential leading man on the Hallmark Channel, as well as through acclaimed dramatic roles in HBO's True Blood, Hart of Dixie, and Deception, Amanda has built an equally inspiring behind-the-scenes career within premier entertainment studios, notably Disney-ABC Television Group.

Throughout their eighteen years of marriage, Amanda and Wes Brown have earned widespread admiration throughout Hollywood as one of the most stable, genuine couples in the industry. Balancing demanding studio production schedules with active parenthood, Amanda Moye Brown embodies modern creative resilience and family dedication.

Who Is Amanda Moye Brown?

Amanda Moye Brown is a Florida-born entertainment industry professional based in Los Angeles, California. With specialized experience in television music administration, studio project coordination, and live event production, she has contributed to high-profile studio productions under Disney Television Animation and ABC Studios while serving as the creative confidante behind Wes Brown's prolific screen career.

Early Life, Background, and Age

Amanda Moye Brown was born in 1983 in Satellite Beach, Florida. Growing up in coastal Brevard County, she developed a deep affinity for live theatre, vocal performance, and musical storytelling. Her warm Southern upbringing and natural organizational acumen guided her toward creative production studies.

As of 2026, Amanda Moye Brown is 43 years old. Early in her post-collegiate career, her performance talents led her to work as a character performer at Walt Disney World in Orlando, Florida, where she memorably brought classic Disney heroines such as Belle from Beauty and the Beast and Princess Jasmine from Aladdin to life for thousands of visiting park guests.

This early foundation inside the Disney creative ecosystem gave Amanda an intimate appreciation of live theatrical pacing, child audiences, and studio storytelling, which paved the way for her subsequent transition to executive production offices in Los Angeles.

Career at Disney-ABC Television Group and Music Coordination

After relocating to Southern California in the mid-2000s, Amanda Moye Brown transitioned into studio administration, joining the prestigious Disney-ABC Television Group in Burbank, California. Serving as an executive assistant and music production coordinator, she worked alongside studio composers, music supervisors, and animation directors.

Her technical credits include contributing to celebrated animated television projects and feature specials. She worked within the music department for Disney+'s feature animation Phineas and Ferb the Movie: Candace Against the Universe (2020) and the acclaimed Pixar-spinoff series Monsters at Work (2021). Her responsibilities encompassed licensing tracking, session scheduling for orchestral scoring, and vocal talent coordination across demanding broadcast deadlines.

In addition to her formal studio roles, Amanda has frequently served as a vital sounding board for her husband's television projects. Wes Brown has credited Amanda's real-life experiences and sharp sense of humor with inspiring key character interactions in his popular Hallmark original holiday movies.

Marriage to Wes Brown: Romance, Baton Rouge Wedding, and Longtime Bond

The love story between Amanda Moye and Wes Brown began during the mid-2000s in Los Angeles, where both were navigating early careers in television and film. Wes, born in Fort Worth, Texas and raised in Baton Rouge, Louisiana, was immediately drawn to Amanda's vibrant Southern warmth, quick wit, and shared Christian values.

After several years of steady dating, the couple officially tied the knot on May 2, 2008, in a joyful, traditional ceremony in Baton Rouge, Louisiana. Surrounded by family, Louisiana State University alumni, and close entertainment friends, the wedding marked the beginning of an eighteen-year marriage that has remained remarkably free of Hollywood turbulence.

Shortly after their wedding, Wes Brown's acting career exploded with a breakthrough recurring role as Luke McDonald on HBO's cultural phenomenon True Blood, followed by leading roles on The CW's Hart of Dixie, ABC's Once Upon a Time (as Gaston), and over a dozen beloved Hallmark holiday features including Christmas at Graceland and Haul Out the Holly. Through every career transition, Amanda remained his steadfast anchor, often joining him on location shoots across the country while managing her own production commitments.

Their long-standing partnership mirrors the enduring Hollywood unions highlighted in our profiles of Diane Addonizio and Armstead Edwards, demonstrating that mutual support and shared priorities are the cornerstones of lasting fulfillment.

Children and Family Life: Raising Daughter Merribeth

In September 2009, Amanda and Wes Brown welcomed their first and only child, a daughter named Merribeth Brown. Now a creative teenager in 2026, Merribeth shares her parents' deep love of music, outdoor sports, and family traditions.

The Brown family resides in a welcoming home in Los Angeles, where they prioritize home-cooked Southern meals, church community activities, and frequent trips back to Louisiana and Florida to spend time with extended family. Wes Brown frequently posts heartfelt tributes honoring Amanda on Mother's Day and their wedding anniversaries, openly thanking her for shaping their home with grace, laughter, and unconditional love.

Amanda Moye Brown Net Worth and Financial Standing

Through her career as an entertainment coordinator, Disney-ABC music department assistant, and production professional, Amanda Moye Brown has earned an estimated personal net worth of $1 Million to $1.5 Million.

Together with her husband Wes Brown, whose steady two-decade career across network television, Hallmark movies, and commercial voice work has yielded substantial earnings, the family's combined net worth is estimated between $5 Million and $8 Million. Their financial portfolio includes prime Los Angeles residential real estate, investments in entertainment development, and active charitable contributions to animal rescue foundations and pediatric hospital programs.

Where Is Amanda Moye Brown Today?

As of 2026, Amanda Moye Brown continues to thrive in Los Angeles alongside Wes and their daughter Merribeth. She remains active in freelance production coordination, creative consulting, and supporting Hallmark community events such as the annual Television Critics Association (TCA) press tours.

With their daughter approaching university years and Wes continuing to star in top-rated cable and streaming series, Amanda continues to balance behind-the-scenes professional leadership with a grounded family life that remains an inspiration to fans and peers alike.
