Anya Robbie: Inside Margot Robbie’s Sister’s Life, Career, and Family Story

A detailed biographical look at Anya Robbie: her Queensland childhood, accounting career, close relationship with sister Margot Robbie, and intentional life away from Hollywood cameras.

By Marcus Vance
Chief Investigative Journalist & Senior Biographer
9 min read November 2, 2026

Anya Robbie is an Australian accountant and the eldest sister of Oscar-nominated actress and producer Margot Robbie. Born in Queensland and raised by their single mother, physiotherapist Sarie Kessler, Anya chose to build a quiet career in corporate accounting and financial administration rather than pursue entertainment industry fame.

While Margot Robbie reached the pinnacle of global cinema with landmark roles in The Wolf of Wall Street, I, Tonya, and Barbie, Anya has maintained a private and grounded lifestyle. Her daily focus remains centered on professional finance, family loyalty, and her Queensland community.

Similar to other relatives of world-renowned figures—such as Yasmin Abdallah prioritizing personal creative integrity away from tabloid glare or Cody Herpin managing life outside celebrity headlines—Anya Robbie demonstrates how family stability and quiet accomplishment thrive far outside the red carpet.

Who Is Anya Robbie?

Anya Robbie is widely recognized by the public as the older sister of global film star Margot Robbie. However, her personal identity is grounded in her own professional achievements as a qualified accountant in Australia.

Unlike many celebrity siblings who capitalize on family connections by launching lifestyle brands or pursuing public-facing roles, Anya has consistently avoided media attention. She rarely conducts media interviews or maintains public social media profiles.

Those close to the Robbie family describe Anya as an analytical, dependable, and deeply thoughtful person. Her practical perspective has provided an important emotional anchor for her younger sister throughout Margot's rapid ascent in the film industry.

Early Life, Queensland Roots, and Sibling Dynamic

Anya grew up alongside her three siblings on the scenic Gold Coast and at their grandparents' farm in the rural town of Dalby, Queensland. Their upbringing was shaped significantly by the dedication of their mother, Sarie Kessler, who raised the four children as a single parent after separating from their father, Doug Robbie.

Family life in regional Queensland taught the siblings the value of hard work, self-reliance, and mutual cooperation. Sarie worked long hours as a physiotherapist to provide for the household, while the older children took on practical responsibilities around the home.

Anya naturally stepped into a nurturing and organizing role as the eldest daughter. That early sense of responsibility helped cultivate the meticulous organization and steady focus that later defined her career in corporate finance.

Anya Robbie alongside her mother Sarie Kessler and sister Margot Robbie, highlighting their enduring Queensland family connection.

Education and Professional Career in Accounting

Following her secondary school education in Queensland, Anya enrolled in university coursework focusing on commerce, accounting, and business administration. She showed a clear aptitude for numeracy, balance sheets, and organizational governance.

After completing her qualifications, she entered the corporate workforce as an accountant. In her professional practice, she has managed financial ledgers, corporate audits, tax compliance, and business advisory services for Australian firms.

Her choice of career reflects a deliberate personal preference for concrete, structured problem-solving over the unpredictable nature of creative performance arts. Colleagues know her for her integrity, professional discretion, and thorough technical competence.

The Robbie Siblings: Lachlan, Cameron, and Margot

The Robbie household produced four distinct career paths. The eldest brother, Lachlan Robbie, pursued stunt work and technical production in the film industry, contributing behind the scenes to major Hollywood releases filmed in Australia and abroad.

The youngest sibling, Cameron Robbie, entered the performing arts as an actor and entertainment television presenter. He has appeared in Australian independent short films and conducted red carpet interviews for Australian network television.

Margot, of course, became one of the most bankable and respected performers of her generation, earning Academy Award nominations and launching her production company, LuckyChap Entertainment. Despite their different career choices, all four siblings remain tightly knit and communicate regularly.

Margot Robbie’s Breakthrough and Anya’s Support

When Margot relocated to Melbourne at age seventeen to join the cast of the classic Australian soap opera Neighbours, her family provided continuous emotional encouragement. Anya was among the first to offer practical guidance as Margot navigated independent living.

Later, as Margot made the leap to Los Angeles and landed her breakout role opposite Leonardo DiCaprio in Martin Scorsese's 2013 film The Wolf of Wall Street, Anya traveled to New York to support her sister at the premiere. Photographers captured rare images of Anya celebrating Margot's artistic milestone.

Margot has frequently mentioned in major interviews how her sister Anya and her family prevent Hollywood glamour from altering her values. When Margot returns home to Australia, family gatherings revolve around home cooking, outdoor activities, and shared childhood memories rather than box office figures.

Choosing Privacy Over Hollywood Fame

In an era dominated by influencer culture and digital self-promotion, Anya Robbie's decision to maintain strict personal privacy is notable. She has chosen not to run verified social media pages or monetize her sister's international fame.

This deliberate boundary allows Anya to live an authentic, normal life in Australia without the constant scrutiny of paparazzi or online commentators. Friends note that she values genuine relationships and peace of mind above public recognition.

Her grounded mindset mirrors that of other notable figures such as Nicolo Robert Tucci, who also embraced personal creative focus over mainstream Hollywood publicity.

Estimated Net Worth and Financial Independence

Through her long-standing professional career in corporate accountancy and financial management, Anya Robbie has attained solid financial independence. Industry estimates place her personal net worth between $600,000 and $900,000 USD.

Her wealth is derived from sustained corporate employment, sensible savings, and private investments rather than studio contracts or corporate brand partnerships. This financial autonomy reflects the strong work ethic instilled by her mother during their upbringing.

Summary and Lasting Perspective

Anya Robbie represents a compelling example of individuality within a high-profile family. Rather than allowing her sister's global celebrity to define her life, she chose a demanding career in finance and built a successful professional reputation on her own terms.

Her steadfast commitment to privacy, family loyalty, and personal career goals has preserved the close bond she shares with Margot, Lachlan, and Cameron. As the Robbie family continues to thrive across different fields, Anya remains a respected and grounding figure at its heart.
