Italia Anita Maria Smith: LL Cool J's Daughter, Cornell Education, Career, and Family Life

Inside the life of James Todd Smith's eldest daughter, her commercial real estate career, Northeastern and Cornell studies, and marriage to Lamar Cardinez.

By Sarah Jenkins
Chief Investigative Journalist & Senior Biographer
8 min read November 9, 2026

In an era where the children of entertainment icons frequently leverage their famous surnames to amass quick social media followings and lifestyle sponsorship deals, Italia Anita Maria Smith represents a refreshing, deeply admirable counter-narrative. As the eldest daughter of two-time Grammy Award-winning hip-hop trailblazer and NCIS: Los Angeles star LL Cool J (James Todd Smith) and jewelry designer Simone I. Smith, Italia was raised with the understanding that privilege is never a substitute for education, personal accountability, and independent professional competence.

Rather than pursuing acting or musical recording contracts, Italia channeled her ambitions into higher education and corporate achievement, earning credentials from Northeastern University and Cornell University before forging a successful, licensed career in commercial real estate and marketing. Today, she balances professional leadership with devoted motherhood, standing as an inspiring example of her parents' principled approach to family life.

Growing Up in Long Island: Grounded Parenting in the Smith Household

Italia Anita Maria Smith was born on January 3, 1991, in Long Island, New York. She is the second of four children born to LL Cool J and Simone Smith, whose enduring love story began in their teenage years in Queens during the mid-1980s. Italia grew up alongside her older brother, photographer and creative director Najee Smith (born 1989), and younger sisters Samaria Smith (born 1995) and Nina-Symone Smith (born 2000).

Despite LL Cool J's status as a pioneer who shaped the global hip-hop revolution with legendary albums like Radio, Bigger and Deffer, and Mama Said Knock You Out, the Smith household was governed by firm rules. Both James and Simone made it explicitly clear that their wealth belonged to the parents—not the children—and that each child had to pursue a higher education and earn their own financial independence.

“I told my kids early on: 'I'm rich, you're not. You have to get an education, find your own passion, and build your own life.' If they wanted something, they had to work for it, because real self-worth only comes from what you earn with your own two hands.”

— LL Cool J, Oprah Prime Television Interview (2013)

This disciplined, affectionate home environment shielded Italia and her siblings from the destructive entitlement that often afflicts celebrity households, fostering a work ethic that became the bedrock of her subsequent career.

Academic Rigor: Northeastern University and Cornell Real Estate Credentials

Upon completing high school with academic honors, Italia enrolled at Northeastern University in Boston, Massachusetts. At Northeastern, renowned for its competitive business curriculum and rigorous cooperative education programs, she pursued a Bachelor of Science in Business Administration with dual concentrations in Business Management and Marketing.

During her collegiate years, Italia immersed herself in practical corporate internships, developing a keen analytical eye for commercial leasing, consumer demographic shifts, and brand positioning. Recognized by professors for her focus and collaborative leadership, she graduated with honors, determined to apply her knowledge in competitive real-world industries.

Recognizing the dynamic potential of metropolitan property markets, Italia continued her education through the prestigious real estate program at Cornell University, earning professional certification in Commercial Real Estate. This specialized training equipped her with sophisticated skills in financial modeling, asset valuation, contract negotiation, and property development.

Corporate Independence: Building a Career Beyond Celebrity Influence

Armed with top-tier credentials from Northeastern and Cornell, Italia launched her career in corporate marketing and commercial property advisory. Rather than stepping into entertainment management or leveraging her father's industry Rolodex, she entered competitive corporate environments where professional accountability and quantitative execution are paramount.

Her early professional journey included strategic roles at CBS Corporation, where she worked in digital marketing, social media strategy, and corporate communications. Handling high-volume corporate communications taught her the nuances of multi-platform branding, audience segmentation, and high-stakes media management. Building upon that foundation, she transitioned into licensed commercial real estate, advising commercial clients on property acquisition, retail leasing, and asset optimization across dynamic metropolitan New York submarkets.

In the commercial property sector, transactions depend strictly on financial modeling, zoning compliance, lease structuring, and fiduciary integrity. Institutional landlords and corporate tenants evaluate brokers solely on their market knowledge and transactional execution. By consistently delivering successful outcomes for her clients, Italia firmly established her reputation on professional merit, proving that true credibility is built through individual competence and diligence.

The Smith Sibling Dynamic: Shared Values and Individual Paths

The grounded ethos instilled by LL Cool J and Simone Smith shaped the trajectories of all four Smith children, creating a tight-knit sibling circle where each individual pursued distinct passions. Italia's older brother, Najee Smith, developed into a celebrated professional photographer and creative director, capturing high-end editorial and fine-art portraits with an eye for cultural authenticity.

Her younger sister, Samaria Smith, graduated from the Fashion Institute of Technology (FIT) in New York City, founding her own successful denim and ready-to-wear lifestyle brand, Southern Girl Goods, which champions ethical production and body-inclusive design. The youngest sibling, Nina-Symone Smith, embraced music, releasing contemporary R&B singles under her artist moniker Nina-Symone while pursuing classical vocal development. Despite their varying creative fields, the four siblings maintain a deeply affectionate, mutually supportive bond, regularly gathering for Sunday family dinners and celebrating each other's milestones away from public fanfare.

Marriage to Lamar Cardinez and Dedicated Motherhood

While attending Northeastern University, Italia met Lamar Cardinez, a fellow student whose ambition, grounded values, and warmth complemented her own. What began as a collegiate romance developed into a deep, lasting partnership over several years of mutual professional growth.

On June 17, 2017, Italia and Lamar were united in holy matrimony during an exquisite ceremony held at the historic First Baptist Church in Glen Cove, Long Island, followed by an elegant, star-studded reception. LL Cool J proudly walked his eldest daughter down the aisle in a custom white bridal gown, sharing an emotional father-daughter dance that became an unforgettable family highlight.

In the years since their wedding, Italia and Lamar have welcomed three children: son King, daughter Faith, and son Noah. Italia has spoken openly about the profound fulfillment of motherhood, maintaining private family routines while managing her real estate and consulting ventures with grace and intentional balance.

Supporting Family Causes: Standing Alongside Simone Smith's Cancer Advocacy

Beyond her professional and domestic responsibilities, Italia is an active supporter of her family's extensive philanthropic initiatives. In 2004, her mother Simone Smith was diagnosed with stage III chondrosarcoma, a rare form of bone cancer. The family rallied together through grueling surgeries and intensive rehabilitation, emerging from the crisis with an even deeper bond of solidarity.

Simone's successful recovery inspired the launch of the Simone I. Smith jewelry collection in partnership with the American Cancer Society, raising millions of dollars for cancer research and patient support services. Italia frequently appears alongside her mother and sisters at charitable galas and cancer awareness symposiums, using her business expertise to amplify community outreach and champion preventative healthcare access.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Italia Anita Maria Smith's profession?

Italia is a licensed commercial real estate professional and corporate marketing executive. She holds a Bachelor of Science in Business Administration from Northeastern University and advanced commercial real estate certification from Cornell University.

Who is Italia Smith married to?

Italia married Lamar Cardinez on June 17, 2017, in Long Island, New York. The couple met while studying at Northeastern University and now have three children together.

Who are Italia Smith's parents and siblings?

Italia is the eldest daughter of hip-hop pioneer and actor LL Cool J (James Todd Smith) and jewelry designer Simone I. Smith. Her siblings are brother Najee Smith and sisters Samaria Smith and Nina-Symone Smith.

Does Italia Smith work in the entertainment industry?

No. Unlike many celebrity children, Italia chose not to pursue acting or music, deliberately building an independent corporate career in business marketing and commercial property transactions.
