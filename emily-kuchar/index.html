Who Is Emily Kuchar? The Biography of Zack Greinke’s Wife, Her Age, Cheerleading, and Family

An in-depth profile of former NFL cheerleader and pageant titleholder Emily Kuchar: her high school romance with Cy Young pitcher Zack Greinke, motherhood, and life today.

By Marcus Vance
Senior Cultural Affairs Editor
9 min read October 21, 2026

In the high-stakes realm of Major League Baseball, where starting pitchers endure rigorous six-month travel schedules and relentless media scrutiny, Zack Greinke has long been celebrated as one of the most uniquely brilliant minds ever to step onto the mound. From winning the 2009 American League Cy Young Award with the Kansas City Royals to earning six All-Star selections and six Gold Glove Awards across historic tenures with the Dodgers, Diamondbacks, and Astros, Greinke established himself as a future Hall of Famer. Yet throughout his entire two-decade professional odyssey, his steadiest source of personal support has been his high school sweetheart and wife, Emily Kuchar Greinke.

An accomplished athlete and performer in her own right, Emily was a former Dallas Cowboys cheerleader and state beauty pageant titleholder before transitioning into motherhood and boutique creative styling. Her unwavering loyalty and deep understanding of athletic pressures have helped make the Greinke household an oasis of calm away from stadium noise.

An Overview of Emily Kuchar’s Life

Emily Kuchar is an American former dancer, professional cheerleader, and digital lifestyle curator celebrated as the life partner of Major League Baseball icon Zack Greinke. Recognized for her radiant poise and athletic background, Emily established her own national credentials prior to marriage by performing on elite cheer squads and earning prestigious beauty titles.

Much like other grounded spouses of prominent sports superstars covered on our platform—such as Juanita Vanoy and Mia Weier—Emily’s relationship with Zack is rooted in shared youth and genuine emotional partnership rather than public glamour. Their enduring marriage has navigated team trades, cross-country relocations, and high-pressure playoff series with admirable composure.

Birth Date, Florida Roots, and Exact Age

Emily Kuchar was born on December 11, 1986, in Daytona Beach, Florida. As of 2026, Emily is 39 years old and will celebrate her milestone 40th birthday in December. Raised in central Florida, she discovered a passion for dance, gymnastics, and performance arts at an early age, training rigorously in classical ballet, jazz, and competitive cheerleading.

During her adolescence, she attended Apopka High School in Orange County, Florida. It was in the hallways and library of Apopka High that her destiny intertwined with that of an unassuming, hyper-focused teenage baseball phenomenon named Zack Greinke.

High School Sweethearts: Meeting Zack Greinke in Florida

Emily and Zack met during their teenage years at Apopka High School, where Zack was the star shortstop and pitcher for the Blue Darters varsity squad, and Emily was a prominent member of the school cheerleading and dance team. According to interviews with close high school acquaintances, Zack was a quiet, introverted student, while Emily was outgoing and energetic. Their contrasting personalities formed an immediate balance.

Emily worked as a student library assistant, and Zack frequently spent his study periods in the library to speak with her. Their teenage romance blossomed naturally into a committed partnership that weathered Zack’s selection as the 6th overall pick in the first round of the 2002 Major League Baseball draft straight out of high school.

Professional Cheerleading and Pageant Accolades

While Zack was ascending through the minor leagues toward his Kansas City Royals debut, Emily pursued her own educational and artistic ambitions. She enrolled at the University of Central Florida in Orlando, focusing on communications and visual arts.

Possessing elite dance training, Emily auditioned for the world-renowned Dallas Cowboys Cheerleaders (DCC), earning a coveted spot on the premier cheer squad in the National Football League. Performing in front of eighty thousand fans at Texas Stadium, Emily honed her stage presence and discipline. Following her NFL cheer tenure, she competed in pageantry, winning the prestigious title of Miss Daytona Beach USA 2009 and placing highly in the Miss Florida USA competition.

Their 2009 Wedding and Navigating the Major League Baseball Circuit

The year 2009 was transformative for the couple. On the field, Zack posted a historic 2.16 ERA and captured the AL Cy Young Award. Off the field, the couple sealed their decade-long romance by marrying on November 21, 2009, in a joyful church ceremony surrounded by family and close athletic colleagues.

Over the subsequent decade, Emily stood by Zack as his career took him through multiple major markets, including Milwaukee, Anaheim, Los Angeles (Dodgers), Arizona (Diamondbacks), and Houston (Astros). Emily's steady presence was particularly instrumental during early chapters of Zack's career when he candidly addressed social anxiety, helping him navigate public responsibilities while preserving mental health.

Motherhood and Raising Three Sons

The Greinke family grew with the arrival of three sons, each of whom has inherited their parents’ passion for outdoor sports and athletics:

  • Bode Nicholas Greinke: Born on July 23, 2015 (age 11 in 2026).
  • Koby Stewart Greinke: Born on November 8, 2017 (age 9 in 2026).
  • Jordy Richard Greinke: Born in November 2021 (age 5 in 2026).

As a mother, Emily has embraced the challenges of managing three active young boys while balancing the nomadic demands of professional sports. She frequently shares wholesome snapshots of Little League baseball games, family golf afternoons, and community charity events, keeping her family grounded and appreciative of their blessings.

Emily Kuchar’s Net Worth and Philanthropic Initiatives

As of 2026, Emily Kuchar possesses an estimated personal net worth of approximately $1.5 Million through past commercial modeling contracts, dance instruction, and independent lifestyle ventures. When coupled with Zack Greinke’s career baseball earnings—which include record-setting contracts such as his $206.5 million deal with the Diamondbacks—their combined family fortune exceeds $120 Million.

The Greinkes have channeled substantial resources into charitable philanthropy. They actively support youth sports programs in Florida and Arizona, contribute to children’s hospital foundations, and participate in animal rescue causes, reflecting their commitment to giving back to communities that supported their journey.

Where Is Emily Kuchar Today?

Today, Emily Kuchar Greinke lives a fulfilling, private life primarily divided between their residences in Florida and Arizona. Following Zack’s legendary major league career, the couple enjoys uninterrupted family time, focusing on their sons' schooling, youth sports tournaments, and peaceful outdoor living.

Her story remains a shining testament to the power of high school sweethearts growing together through extraordinary success while keeping core family values intact.
