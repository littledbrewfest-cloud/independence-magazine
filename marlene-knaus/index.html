Who Is Marlene Knaus? The Biography of Niki Lauda's First Wife, Career, and Life Today

From European fashion runways in the 1970s to supporting Formula One champion Niki Lauda and raising their two sons.

By Sarah Jenkins
Senior Biographical Correspondent
7 min read October 11, 2026

In the high-octane world of 1970s international motorsport, few figures carried the elegance and quiet poise of Marlene Knaus. Known throughout Europe as a successful fashion model, she became a central presence in Formula One during the peak championship years of Austrian racing icon Niki Lauda.

While the racing world focused on lap times and rivalries, Knaus provided stability away from the paddock. Her partnership with Lauda spanned fifteen years, through world championships, career milestones, and the raising of two sons who continue to honor their parents' legacy.

Early Life and Modeling Career

Marlene Knaus was born in 1956 into a family of Chilean and Austrian descent. Raised between South America and Europe, she developed an appreciation for fashion, photography, and the arts from an early age.

By the early 1970s, Knaus had established herself as a sought-after model in Paris, Milan, and Vienna. Her distinctive style, characterized by natural elegance and an effortlessly chic wardrobe, made her a frequent fixture in European editorial magazines. Her social circle included prominent artists, designers, and public figures across the European continent.

Meeting Niki Lauda and Their 1976 Marriage

Knaus met Niki Lauda in the mid-1970s at a private dinner party in Salzburg, Austria. At the time, Lauda was driving for Scuderia Ferrari and rapidly ascending toward his first Formula One World Championship.

The couple formed a close bond grounded in shared values and mutual respect. In 1976, they married in a quiet civil ceremony in Austria, keeping the occasion deliberately low-key despite immense media interest.

Support During Lauda's Career and Recovery

In August 1976, just months after their wedding, Lauda suffered a severe crash at the German Grand Prix at the Nürburgring. During his weeks in the hospital and his remarkable return to the cockpit just six weeks later at Monza, Knaus remained by his side as his principal caregiver and emotional pillar.

In his later autobiographies, Lauda credited Knaus's calm demeanor and practical support with helping him navigate one of the most demanding periods of his professional life. She traveled with him to races around the globe while ensuring their home life in Austria remained grounded and peaceful.

Family Life: Lukas and Mathias Lauda

The couple welcomed two sons together: Lukas Lauda, born in 1979, and Mathias Lauda, born in 1981. Lukas later pursued sports management, serving as his brother's manager, while Mathias followed in his father's footsteps, developing a professional career in sports car racing and competing in the FIA World Endurance Championship.

Knaus emphasized a balanced, private upbringing for her sons, shielding them from intrusive media attention during their formative years.

Amicable Divorce and Enduring Friendship

In 1991, after fifteen years of marriage, Marlene Knaus and Niki Lauda finalized an amicable divorce. Unlike many high-profile celebrity splits, their separation was marked by cordiality and mutual respect.

The two remained close friends and active co-parents for the rest of Lauda's life. When Lauda later married Birgit Wetzinger in 2008, Knaus maintained warm relations with the extended family, regularly attending family gatherings and celebrations in Ibiza and Vienna.

Life Today and Net Worth

Today, Marlene Knaus lives a serene, private life, dividing her time between residences in Ibiza, Spain, and Austria. She rarely participates in public interviews, preferring to focus on her family, art collections, and philanthropy.

Her net worth is estimated between $3 Million and $5 Million, reflecting her modeling earnings, divorce settlement, and conservative European property investments.
